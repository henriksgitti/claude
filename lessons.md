- 2026-11-07 — For Bavarian grant calls, the Bayern Innovativ *downloads* subpage (`…/fluegge/fluegge-downloads/`) links the current call PDF (e.g. `foerderaufruf-fluegge-2026.pdf`), while the programme page only says "open through …"; numbers change between calls (FLÜGGE: 6 → 4 projects), so never carry them over from the previous call. - 2026-11-07 — For national payouts of care-insurance grants (§ 8 Abs. 7/8 SGB XI), the BAS 'Finanzierungsanteile PKV/GKV' PDFs list the yearly total per programme (the base for the PKV share), so you do not need an insurer press release or a Bundestag question. Interest-group worked examples can use different legal readings (DBfK applies the § 113e 10 % to all qualified posts, FINSOZ to the corridor only); quote both and say which one matches the law text. Bill reasoning can lag the law text (PNOG general part still says 'teilstationär'): cite the law text. (context: A15)
- 2026-11-07 — A knock-out written as "out only if *no* route exists" is inert when the routes are legally always possible and invisible in the data (below-threshold and private purchases are not in TED). Write knock-outs so that passing needs evidence (a named example of the route in use), and turn missing evidence into a confidence penalty, not a pass. Also: never put the same word ("inference") in two confidence levels; the scorer will pick the milder one. Statutory insurers' direct-award limit is § 22 Abs. 2 SVHV (≤€50k net), not the Land rule. (context: red team v2 of research/scoring.md)
- 2026-11-07 — EUR-Lex now answers plain curl (the full MDR text in German came back as 2 MB HTML), so primary EU law no longer has to rest on law-firm summaries. For conflicts of interest in open-access papers, Europe PMC `/<PMCID>/fullTextXML` contains the COI and funding sections. In TED, about 1 in 4 notices give no thresholds in `selection-criterion-description-lot` ("siehe Teilnahmeunterlagen" or empty): mark those "unknown", do not assume they are open. Sentence-splitting German notice text on "." breaks at "Mind." and "gem.", so print a window around the keyword instead. (context: A14)
- 2026-11-07 — The CTIS public search API works without a key: `POST https://euclinicaltrials.eu/ctis-public-api/search` with `{"pagination":{"page":n,"size":100},"sort":{"property":"decisionDate","direction":"DESC"},"searchCriteria":{}}`. Results cap at 10,000, so pull DESC and ASC and de-duplicate by `ctNumber`. `searchCriteria` accepts `isTransitioned` (exclude EudraCT carry-overs) and `msc:[276]` (Germany). `trialCountries` is "Country:statuscode", not a site count. Also read both sides of an interest-group survey: the vfa sponsor survey blames "fehlende Patienten", while the same associations' site survey shows sites barely see patient identification as a hurdle. - 2026-11-07 — TED award notices: query by `procedure-identifier` to chain cn → can, and request `winner-name`, `received-submissions-type-code/-val` (bids, `part-req`), `non-award-justification` (e.g. `chan-need`) and `direct-award-justification-text-proc`. The XML/PDF links on ted.europa.eu are bot-checked (202), but the API fields are enough. Tender documents on DTVP are public without login: the notice field `document-url-lot` gives the project; the xlsx eligibility forms hold the exact turnover/staff/reference thresholds that TED only summarises as 'siehe Vergabeunterlagen'. Check renames: DAVASO = IQVIA Health System Services, so count winners by entity, not by name. Never put a bare `cat` in a scripted shell chain: it waits on stdin and hangs the command. - 2026-11-07 — Before trusting a weighted-sum score, build one adversarial example: give a known-fatal flaw the worst score and everything else plausible highs, then check whether it still outranks a sound idea. Here a no-payer idea won 68 vs 61. Also, "show evidence only for scores ≥4 or ≤2" turns 3 into the place unknowns go unnoticed: require evidence and confidence for every score. Procurement thresholds that decide startup access: Bavaria direct award ≤€100k net (Art. 20 BayWiVG), federal ≤€50k (from 1 Jul 2026), EU tender ≥€216k (2026–27).
