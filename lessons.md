- 2026-11-07 — A knock-out written as "out only if *no* route exists" is inert when the routes are legally always possible and invisible in the data (below-threshold and private purchases are not in TED). Write knock-outs so that passing needs evidence (a named example of the route in use), and turn missing evidence into a confidence penalty, not a pass. Also: never put the same word ("inference") in two confidence levels; the scorer will pick the milder one. Statutory insurers' direct-award limit is § 22 Abs. 2 SVHV (≤€50k net), not the Land rule.
