# Founder brief

Written by the CEO (Henrik). Last updated 2026-11-06. Agents: use this to score opportunities. Do not edit; only the CEO does.

## Goal (CEO's words)
> "Build a company that contributes to tackle future healthcare problems. I want to think big but achievable."
> "A real startup idea, which means thinking about how to get grants, capital, co-founders. Preferably I would like to found in Munich."
> "My goal is to develop bulletproof business ideas which are ready to start."

Interpretation for agents:
- **Big:** large, growing problem (ageing, workforce shortage, chronic disease, cost pressure, access).
- **Achievable:** a small team can reach a first pilot or paying customer within ~12 months; fundable via grants/pre-seed.
- **Ready to start:** evidence-backed, with known risks and kill criteria, not "risk-free".

## Constraints
- **Scope:** healthcare broadly (opened 2026-11-06; previously cancer only).
- **Location:** found in Munich, Germany. First market likely Germany/DACH, then EU.
- **Time:** part-time for now (full-time job). Agents do the research; the CEO decides.
- **Research approach:** desk research first (Reddit, forums, whole internet). Customer interviews later, as validation before founding.
- **Funding:** wants grants, investors, and co-founders. Budget of own money: not stated.

## Profile
- **Education:** Bachelor's in Digital Transformation / Innovation (non-scientific, non-technical); Master's in Innovation, Nova SBE Lisbon, specialization Health Innovation.
- **Work:** Full-time since May 2026 in AI agent implementation (asset management, Lisbon), after an internship there. ~1.5 years of experience.
- **Interests:** healthcare (pharma, medtech, digital health, providers, payers, CROs); especially drug development and clinical research.
- **Languages:** German and English fluent; learning Brazilian Portuguese.

## Founder fit
- **Strengths:** health innovation training; hands-on AI agent / automation implementation; German-speaking (needed for the German healthcare system); international network (Lisbon).
- **Gaps:** no clinical, scientific, or software-engineering background → ideas need a co-founder covering the gap (e.g. CTO, clinician). Avoid ideas whose core is new biology or high-risk medical devices unless the co-founder profile solves it.
- **Sweet spot (hypothesis):** AI/automation for workflow, operations, and access problems in healthcare: e.g. clinical trial operations, care coordination, administrative burden, nursing/staff shortage.
