# Trends & policy scan: Germany/EU healthcare, 2026–2040
Task: A1 | Date: 2026-11-06 | Status: draft

Grades per `CLAUDE.md` rule 2: **[A]** official statistics, ministries, regulators, law/draft-law texts, parliamentary answers; **[B]** news, associations, law firms, industry reports; **[C]** forums (none used here). All sources below were opened (via curl + text extraction, see Sources) unless marked `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## Findings
- **The demographic squeeze is locked in for the next ~12 years.** People aged 67+ in Germany grew from 10.4m (1990) to 16.7m (2024) and will reach "mindestens 20,5 Millionen" by the end of the 2030s; the working-age population (20–66) shrinks by 3.2m–4.9m by 2035 depending on migration [A] ([Destatis 16. kBV, Dec 2025](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Bevoelkerungsvorausberechnung/annahmen_ergebnisse_16te_kBv.html)). More patients, fewer workers: every trend below follows from this.
- **Care (Pflege) is the sharpest pain point.** 5.7m people needed long-term care in Dec 2023 (+15% vs 2021); 86% (4.9m) are cared for at home, 3.1m of them mainly by relatives [A] ([Destatis via Presseportal](https://www.presseportal.de/pm/32102/5933248)). Germany will be short 280,000–690,000 nurses/carers by 2049, and 90,000–350,000 as early as 2034 [A] ([Destatis, Jan 2024](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2024/01/PD24_033_23_12.html)). Nursing-home residents pay on average €3,364/month themselves in year 1 (+€256 in a year) [B] ([vdek, 14 Jul 2026](https://www.vdek.com/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/stationaere-pflege-eigenanteile-juli-2026/_jcr_content/par/download/file.res/vdek_pm_20260714_Eigenbeteiligung_Pflegeheim_final.pdf)).
- **Payers are in a cost-cutting regime, not an innovation-spending regime.** Statutory health insurance (GKV) spending rose 7.8% in 2025 vs 5.3% contribution income; the FinanzKommission sees a €15bn gap in 2027, rising to about €40bn by 2030 [A] ([BMG, 10 Mar 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/finanzentwicklung-gkv-2025); [FKG, 30 Mar 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/F/FinanzKommission_Gesundheit/FKG-PM_Bericht_I_30-03-26.pdf)). The savings law (GKV-BStabG, passed 10 Jul 2026) needs "fast 19 Milliarden Euro" in 2027 and caps provider price increases at "Grundlohnrate minus 1 Prozentpunkt" for 2027–2029 [A] ([BMG, 10 Jul 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/ministerium/meldungen/bundestag-beschliesst-gkv-beitragssatzstabilisierunggesetz-pm-10-07-2026)). Long-term care insurance (SPV) faces a €7.6bn gap in 2027 [B] ([Börsen-Zeitung](https://www.boersen-zeitung.de/konjunktur-politik/kabinett-verlagert-strukturreform-der-pflege-in-expertenkommission)).
- **Hospitals are in restructuring and in deficit.** The hospital reform (KHVVG, 5 Dec 2024; adjusted by the KHAG, in force 15 Apr 2026) brings up to €50bn for 2026–2035, of which €29bn comes from the federal government [A] ([BMG, 17 Apr 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/transformationsfonds-bundesamt-fuer-soziale-sicherung-bewilligt-bereits-erste-foerdermittel-17-04-2026)). The Krankenhaus Rating Report 2026 expects 60% of hospitals in deficit in 2027 and ~70% by 2030, with 8.5% of non-nursing full-time posts to be cut by 2030 [B] ([AOK G+G, 25 Jun 2026](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/update/krankenhaeuser-insolvenzen-rating/)).
- **Policy is now funding digitisation in outpatient care and steering patients.** Draft care law (PNOG): €1.6bn for 2027–2031 to digitise outpatient and day-care facilities, covering documentation systems, assistive systems, IT, interfaces and security [A] ([PNOG RefE, 5 Jun 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/P/RefE-Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_PNOG.pdf)). Only 50% of care facilities nationwide had an activated TI card (SMC-B) by April 2026, although connecting to the health data network (TI) has been mandatory since 1 Jul 2025 [A] ([Berlin Senate answer, 19 May 2026](https://pardok.parlament-berlin.de/starweb/adis/citat/VT/19/SchrAnfr/S19-25964.pdf)). The emergency-care reform (passed 15 Oct 2026) builds acute-care call centres on 116117 and links them with 112 [A] ([BMG, 15 Oct 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/bundestag-beschliesst-notfallreform)). The digital law GeDIG adds ePA-based triage plus booking, and e-referrals become mandatory from 1 Sep 2029 [B] ([AOK](https://www.aok.de/pp/gesetz/gesetz-fuer-daten-und-digitale-innovation-im-gesundheitswesen/)).
- **The health-data infrastructure exists but is barely used, which is a gap.** The ePA (electronic patient record) is created for ~73m insured people, but only 5.85m (~8%) use it actively [B] ([zm-online / BfDI, Sep 2026](https://www.zm-online.de/news/detail/rund-acht-prozent-der-gkv-versicherten-nutzt-die-epa-aktiv)). The research data centre FDZ (opened Oct 2025) had 53 applications by Feb 2026 [B] ([PZ](https://www.pharmazeutische-zeitung.de/53-antraege-auf-datennutzung-fuer-forschung-163005/)). The EU Health Data Space (EHDS) phases in from 2027, with the main obligations from March 2029 and 2031 [A] ([European Commission](https://health.ec.europa.eu/ehealth-digital-health-and-care/european-health-data-space-regulation-ehds_en)).
- **The DiGA app-on-prescription route is under pressure.** 1.6m DiGA were used and ~€400m was spent from 2020 to 2025; only 58 DiGA remained listed at end-2025 (16 delisted). The payers' association and the FinanzKommission want negotiated prices from day 1 and admission only with proven benefit [A] ([GKV-SV, 8 Apr 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/gkv_spitzenverband/presse/pressemitteilungen_und_statements/pressemitteilung_2239872.jsp)). *Inference:* for a 2027 start, B2B sales to providers funded by public programmes look like a safer route than DiGA reimbursement.

GPs: 55,778 (+343), yet full GP seats −0.1%; GP average age 55.0 [B] ([PZ / KBV data, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.pharmazeutische-zeitung.de/mehr-aerzte-aber-weniger-zeit-fuer-patienten-163553/)).

### 5. Hospital reform (KHVVG → KHAG)
- KHVVG of 5 Dec 2024. The KHAG ("Reform der Reform", passed by the Bundestag 6 Mar 2026, in force 15 Apr 2026) keeps the service-group logic (Leistungsgruppen, cut from 65 to 61), widens exceptions (time-limited assignments without meeting quality criteria) and **postpones the volume-independent "Vorhaltevergütung" funding by one year** [B] ([Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/de/en/industries/life-sciences/khag-im-bundestag-beschlossen)); [A] ([BMG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/transformationsfonds-bundesamt-fuer-soziale-sicherung-bewilligt-bereits-erste-foerdermittel-17-04-2026)).
- **Transformation fund:** up to €50bn for 2026–2035. The federal share of €29bn comes from the infrastructure fund (replacing the GKV share). The Länder co-fund ≥30% (2026–2029) and ≥50% (2030–2035) of eligible costs. The first grants were approved 17 Apr 2026 [A] ([BMG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/transformationsfonds-bundesamt-fuer-soziale-sicherung-bewilligt-bereits-erste-foerdermittel-17-04-2026)).
- **Finances:** GKV hospital spending +9.6% (+€9.7bn) in 2025, the main cost driver [A] ([BMG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/finanzentwicklung-gkv-2025)). The Rating Report 2026 (555 annual accounts of 864 hospitals) projects 60% in deficit in 2027 and ~70% in 2030; 400 sites are at risk medium-term; 22% of municipal hospitals have owners with unsustainable debt [B] ([AOK G+G](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/update/krankenhaeuser-insolvenzen-rating/)). DKG: hospitals are ready to "auf mehrere Hundert Standorte zu verzichten" over 10 years [B] ([DKG, 15 Jan 2026](https://dkgev.de/fileadmin/default/Mediapool/1_DKG/1.7_Presse/1.7.1_Pressemitteilungen/2026/2026-01-15_PM_DKG-Jahresauftaktpressekonferenz.pdf)).
- **BStabG effects on hospitals:** price cap (Grundlohnrate −1 pp, 2027–29); mandatory staffing-assessment tools for nursing and physicians dropped (the PPBV ordinance and its commission are abolished); **audit rates for hospital bills raised** (40% audit rate if 50–65% of bills go unchallenged; unlimited below 50%) [A] ([BMG BStabG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/ministerium/meldungen/bundestag-beschliesst-gkv-beitragssatzstabilisierunggesetz-pm-10-07-2026)).
- **Shift to outpatient care:** outpatient surgery spending +15.7% in 2025, driven by the hybrid DRGs introduced in 2024 [A] ([BMG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/finanzentwicklung-gkv-2025)).
- **Who is affected:** ~1,700 hospitals (count `[UNVERIFIED]`), clinical and admin staff, Länder planning authorities.
- **Who pays:** GKV (operating costs via DRG, nursing budget, Vorhaltevergütung), Länder (investment), federal government (transformation fund).
