# Trends & policy scan: Germany/EU healthcare, 2026–2040
Task: A1 (revised by A10: critic fixes from `research/reviews/2026-11-06-trends.md`) | Date: 2026-11-06 | Status: draft

Grades per `CLAUDE.md` rule 2: **[A]** official statistics, ministries, regulators, law/draft-law texts, parliamentary answers; **[B]** news, associations, law firms, industry reports; **[C]** forums (none used here). All sources below were opened (via curl + text extraction, see Sources) unless marked `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## Findings
- **The demographic squeeze is locked in for the next ~12 years.** People aged 67+ in Germany grew from 10.4m (1990) to 16.7m (2024) and will reach "mindestens 20,5 Millionen" by the end of the 2030s; the working-age population (20–66) shrinks by 3.2m–4.9m by 2035 depending on migration [A] ([Destatis 16. kBV, Dec 2025](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Bevoelkerungsvorausberechnung/annahmen_ergebnisse_16te_kBv.html)). More patients, fewer workers: this drives much of what follows, but not all of it. Part of the growth in care need comes from the broader 2017 definition of care need, and hospital deficits are partly policy-driven. Both can be changed by policy (the PNOG tightens assessment for Pflegegrade 1–3, see §2).
- **Care (Pflege) is the sharpest pain point.** 5.7m people needed long-term care in Dec 2023 (+15% vs 2021); 86% (4.9m) are cared for at home, 3.1m of them mainly by relatives; 14% (800,000) live in nursing homes. Destatis says the +730,000 rise since 2021 "geht zum Teil auf den weiter gefassten Pflegebegriff zurück" (demographics alone: ~100,000) [A] ([Destatis via Presseportal](https://www.presseportal.de/pm/32102/5933248)). Germany will be short 280,000–690,000 nurses/carers by 2049, and 90,000–350,000 as early as 2034 [A] ([Destatis, Jan 2024](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2024/01/PD24_033_23_12.html)). Nursing-home residents pay on average €3,364/month themselves in year 1 (+€256 in a year) [B] ([vdek, 14 Jul 2026](https://www.vdek.com/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/stationaere-pflege-eigenanteile-juli-2026/_jcr_content/par/download/file.res/vdek_pm_20260714_Eigenbeteiligung_Pflegeheim_final.pdf)).
- **Payers are in a cost-cutting regime, not an innovation-spending regime.** Statutory health insurance (GKV) spending rose 7.8% in 2025 vs 5.3% contribution income; the FinanzKommission sees a €15bn gap in 2027, rising to about €40bn by 2030 [A] ([BMG, 10 Mar 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/finanzentwicklung-gkv-2025); [FKG, 30 Mar 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/F/FinanzKommission_Gesundheit/FKG-PM_Bericht_I_30-03-26.pdf)). The savings law (GKV-BStabG, passed 10 Jul 2026) needs "fast 19 Milliarden Euro" in 2027 and caps provider price increases at "Grundlohnrate minus 1 Prozentpunkt" for 2027–2029 [A] ([BMG, 10 Jul 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/ministerium/meldungen/bundestag-beschliesst-gkv-beitragssatzstabilisierunggesetz-pm-10-07-2026)). Long-term care insurance (SPV) faces a €7.6bn gap in 2027 [B] ([Börsen-Zeitung](https://www.boersen-zeitung.de/konjunktur-politik/kabinett-verlagert-strukturreform-der-pflege-in-expertenkommission)).
- **Hospitals are in restructuring and in deficit.** The hospital reform (KHVVG, 5 Dec 2024; adjusted by the KHAG, in force 15 Apr 2026) brings up to €50bn for 2026–2035, of which €29bn comes from the federal government [A] ([BMG, 17 Apr 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/transformationsfonds-bundesamt-fuer-soziale-sicherung-bewilligt-bereits-erste-foerdermittel-17-04-2026)). The Krankenhaus Rating Report 2026 expects 60% of hospitals in deficit in 2027 and ~70% by 2030. To absorb the savings law, hospitals "müssten" (would need to) cut about 8.5% of non-nursing full-time posts by 2030: a conditional, not a forecast [B] ([AOK G+G, 25 Jun 2026](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/update/krankenhaeuser-insolvenzen-rating/)).
- **Policy plans to fund care-facility digitisation and patient steering; most of it is still draft law.**
  - *Care fund (bill, may change):* the PNOG cabinet draft (30 Sep 2026) sets up €1.6bn for 2027–2031 "für die Digitalisierung ambulanter und stationärer Pflegeeinrichtungen" (the June draft covered outpatient and day care only); the Länder "sollen" co-fund 25% (€400m). Money flows to facilities via the Pflegekassen from 1 Jul 2027; the GKV-SV sets the per-facility rules by 30 Jun 2027 [A] ([PNOG cabinet draft, § 11 Abs. 4–5 SGB XI-new](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/P/Kabinett-GE_Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_PNOG.pdf)). Bundestag 2nd/3rd reading planned 11 Dec 2026 [B] ([AOK PNOG tracker, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.aok.de/pp/gesetz/pnog/)).
  - *Precedent:* an existing grant (§ 8 Abs. 8 SGB XI, 40% of spend, max €12,000 per facility, 2019–2030) has been partly used. DAK, which processes applications for 48% of facilities, reports that ~11,300 of its ~17,500 facilities had used it by mid-2025, but only €81.2m ("knapp 40 Prozent") of the funds was drawn [B] ([DAK, 1 Sep 2025](https://www.dak.de/presse/bundesthemen/politik-unternehmensnachrichten/digitalisierung-70-prozent-der-pflegeeinrichtungen-fehlt-anbindung-an-telematikinfrastruktur_146652)). *My math:* 11,300 / 17,500 = 64.6% of DAK-processed facilities used it at least once. Regionally, few used the full amount: vdek 2024 says only ~30% in Berlin and ~20% in Brandenburg "die Fördersumme ausgeschöpft" [B] ([vdek Berlin](https://www.vdek.com/LVen/BERBRA/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/Berlin/2024/Digitalisierung-in-der-Pflege.html)).
  - *TI lag:* only 50% of care facilities nationwide had an activated TI card (SMC-B) by April 2026, and 40% had applied for a KIM secure-mail address, although TI connection has been mandatory since 1 Jul 2025 [A] ([Berlin Senate answer, 19 May 2026](https://pardok.parlament-berlin.de/starweb/adis/citat/VT/19/SchrAnfr/S19-25964.pdf)).
  - *Steering:* the emergency-care reform (passed 15 Oct 2026) builds acute-care call centres on 116117 and links them with 112 [A] ([BMG, 15 Oct 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/bundestag-beschliesst-notfallreform)). The draft digital law GeDIG adds ePA-based triage plus booking, and practices "sollen" be required to use e-referrals from 1 Sep 2029 (draft; 2nd/3rd reading planned 13 Nov 2026) [B] ([AOK GeDIG tracker, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.aok.de/pp/gesetz/gesetz-fuer-daten-und-digitale-innovation-im-gesundheitswesen/)).
- **The health-data infrastructure exists but is barely used, which is a gap.** The ePA (electronic patient record) is created for ~73m insured people, but only 5.85m (~8%) use it actively [B] ([zm-online / BfDI, Sep 2026](https://www.zm-online.de/news/detail/rund-acht-prozent-der-gkv-versicherten-nutzt-die-epa-aktiv)). The research data centre FDZ (opened Oct 2025) had 53 applications by Feb 2026 [B] ([PZ](https://www.pharmazeutische-zeitung.de/53-antraege-auf-datennutzung-fuer-forschung-163005/)). The EU Health Data Space (EHDS) phases in from 2027, with the main obligations from March 2029 and 2031 [A] ([European Commission](https://health.ec.europa.eu/ehealth-digital-health-and-care/european-health-data-space-regulation-ehds_en)).
- **The DiGA app-on-prescription route is under pressure.** 1.6m DiGA were used and ~€400m was spent from 2020 to 2025; only 58 DiGA remained listed at end-2025 (16 delisted). The payers' association and the FinanzKommission want negotiated prices from day 1 and admission only with proven benefit [A] ([GKV-SV, 8 Apr 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/gkv_spitzenverband/presse/pressemitteilungen_und_statements/pressemitteilung_2239872.jsp)). *Inference:* for a 2027 start, B2B sales to providers look like a safer route than DiGA reimbursement. Caveats: the public programmes are mostly draft law, grant money is not spending (see the § 8 Abs. 8 precedent), and providers under price caps (Grundlohnrate −1 pp) and deficits may also cut software budgets.

### 5. Hospital reform (KHVVG → KHAG)
- KHVVG of 5 Dec 2024. The KHAG ("Reform der Reform", passed by the Bundestag 6 Mar 2026, in force 15 Apr 2026) keeps the service-group logic (Leistungsgruppen, cut from 65 to 61), widens exceptions (time-limited assignments without meeting quality criteria) and **postpones the volume-independent "Vorhaltevergütung" funding by one year** [B] ([Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/de/en/industries/life-sciences/khag-im-bundestag-beschlossen)); [A] ([BMG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/transformationsfonds-bundesamt-fuer-soziale-sicherung-bewilligt-bereits-erste-foerdermittel-17-04-2026)).
- **Transformation fund:** up to €50bn for 2026–2035. The federal share of €29bn comes from the infrastructure fund (replacing the GKV share). The Länder co-fund ≥30% (2026–2029) and ≥50% (2030–2035) of eligible costs. The first grants were approved 17 Apr 2026 [A] ([BMG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/transformationsfonds-bundesamt-fuer-soziale-sicherung-bewilligt-bereits-erste-foerdermittel-17-04-2026)).
- **Finances:** GKV hospital spending +9.6% (+€9.7bn) in 2025, the main cost driver [A] ([BMG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/finanzentwicklung-gkv-2025)). The Rating Report 2026 (555 annual accounts of 864 hospitals) projects 60% in deficit in 2027 and ~70% in 2030; to absorb the savings law, hospitals "müssten" cut ~8.5% of non-nursing FTEs by 2030 (conditional); 400 sites are at risk medium-term; 22% of municipal hospitals have owners with unsustainable debt [B] ([AOK G+G](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/update/krankenhaeuser-insolvenzen-rating/)). DKG: hospitals are ready to "auf mehrere Hundert Standorte zu verzichten" over 10 years [B] ([DKG, 15 Jan 2026](https://dkgev.de/fileadmin/default/Mediapool/1_DKG/1.7_Presse/1.7.1_Pressemitteilungen/2026/2026-01-15_PM_DKG-Jahresauftaktpressekonferenz.pdf)).
- **BStabG effects on hospitals:** price cap (Grundlohnrate −1 pp, 2027–29); mandatory staffing-assessment tools for nursing and physicians dropped (the PPBV ordinance and its commission are abolished); **audit rates for hospital bills raised** (40% audit rate if 50–65% of bills go unchallenged; unlimited below 50%) [A] ([BMG BStabG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/ministerium/meldungen/bundestag-beschliesst-gkv-beitragssatzstabilisierunggesetz-pm-10-07-2026)).
- **Shift to outpatient care:** outpatient surgery spending +15.7% in 2025, driven by the hybrid DRGs introduced in 2024 [A] ([BMG](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/finanzentwicklung-gkv-2025)).
- **Who is affected:** 1,807 hospitals in 2025 (529 public, 549 non-profit, 729 private), with 468,803 beds and 17.7m inpatients [A] ([Destatis PM 376, 28 Oct 2026](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/10/PD26_376_231.html)); clinical and admin staff; Länder planning authorities.
- **Who pays:** GKV (operating costs via DRG, nursing budget, Vorhaltevergütung), Länder (investment), federal government (transformation fund).
