# Consumer-health scan: FUTRUE-like opportunities (self-pay)
Task: C8 (parts 2–3) | Date: 2026-11-14 | Status: draft, **partial** (run 1 of ~2: market frame, adapted scoring, 20 candidates with first evidence; ranking and top-5 sketches not done)

## Findings
- **There is a large self-pay market without any payer contract.** Non-prescription medicines and health products in pharmacies (incl. mail order) sold €12.0bn in 2024 (+7.1 %), of which self-medication ~€10.2bn; with drugstores and supermarkets IQVIA counts €13.8bn and 1.71bn packs ([IQVIA Q4 2024](https://iqvia.com/-/media/iqvia/pdfs/germany/library/publications/iqvia-pharma-marktbericht-classic-q4-2024_fin.pdf); [IQVIA/Healthcare Marketing 4/2025](https://www.iqvia.com/-/media/iqvia/pdfs/germany/publications/artikel-in-der-fachpresse/2025/artikel-otc-geschaeft-waechst-mai-2025.pdf) [B]).
- **Online is where a €10k founder can enter.** OTC mail order grew 10.7 % to €3.57bn in 2024; digestion, supplements and eye products grew fastest (~+10 %) (IQVIA [B]). Supplements sell 67 % of packs outside pharmacies (drugstores, supermarkets) (IQVIA [B]).
- **Scale is proven in this model, unlike in the B2B long list:** PharmaSGP €118.8m revenue, 31 % margin, 30 staff in 2019 (filings [A]); Sunday Natural (Berlin, 2013) EV €800m in 2024 [B]. See `research/futrue-benchmark.md`.
- **Strongest first signals among 20 candidates (not yet ranked):** menopause (9m women aged 40–55 [B]; 57.6 % of women with moderate-severe hot flushes use OTC products [A]; three funded German startups, none a product brand at scale found yet), GLP-1 self-payers (~100,000 private users end-2024 [B], paying up to €276–490 a month [B]; companion products unproven), tinnitus (9.8 % bothersome in a German cohort [A]), female urinary incontinence (48.3 % of women in a German regional survey [A, 2017]).
- **Two traps found:** (1) the €42/month care-supplies box (Pflegebox) is paid by Pflegekassen but is a fraud hotspot with new 2024 rules against cold outreach ([test.de, Oct 2025](https://www.test.de/stiftung-warentest-warnt-pflegeboxen-6249281-0/); [Verbraucherzentrale NRW, Jan 2025](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.nrw/aktuelle-meldungen/gesundheit-pflege/pflegehilfsmittel-zum-verbrauch-warnung-und-neue-regeln-seit-1-juli-95810) [B]); (2) eye drops and gut products that act by substances are class IIa–III devices under MDR Rule 21, out of reach at €10k ([AESGP](https://aesgp.eu/content/uploads/2020/06/AESGP_Position-Paper-Rule-21_March-2020.pdf) [B]).
- **Not done yet:** marketplace sales signals (Amazon blocked: compressed bot response), CAC and margin per category, contract-maker prices, and the scored top 5 (part 3).

### 2. Adapted scoring (proposed; to apply in part 3)
Knock-outs (from task C8):
- **K1 consumer spend proven**: a sourced figure shows consumers in DACH pay for products for this need today (sales, pack counts, a named brand's revenue, or a survey of use of paid products). Prevalence alone does not pass.
- **K2 startable for ≤€50k incl. grants with a contract manufacturer**: route is a food supplement (BVL notification, § 5 NemV [A]), cosmetic, class I device, or a non-medical digital service. Class IIa+ devices, IVDs needing a notified body, drugs (incl. homeopathic registration) and prescription services fail K2 for the start product.

Criteria (1–5 each; weights to be set in part 3, proposal in brackets): need size [A/B] (×2); spend proof (×2); online reach and CAC (×2); competition and incumbent strength (×1); evidence base for the product (×1; mission fit: "tackle healthcare problems"); claims and regulatory risk (×1); margin (×1); founder fit (×1). Confidence tags H/M/L as in `research/scoring.md`.
