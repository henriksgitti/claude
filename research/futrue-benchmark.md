# FUTRUE benchmark: what it is, why it works, what a €10k part-time founder can copy
Task: C8 (part 1 of 3) | Date: 2026-11-14 | Status: draft

## Findings
- **FUTRUE is a founder-owned holding of consumer-health and pharma companies, not a startup studio for outsiders.** Founded 2008 in Gräfelfing by Dr. Clemens Fischer (physician, TUM; Harvard MBA; Novartis Germany executive board 2006–08) and Madlena Hohlefelder (lawyer, HSG economics, BCG). Over 20 companies and "mehr als 90 Arzneimittelzulassungen" (company site [B]; founder CVs in PharmaSGP filings [A]). Core businesses: PharmaSGP (OTC pain, sleep, sexual-weakness brands), SYNformulas (Kijimea probiotics), Vertanical (cannabis drug, approved Jul 2026), plus in-house services (media agency EMCM, CRO, procurement) ([futrue.com](https://www.futrue.com) [B]).
- **The engine: cheap regulatory classes + contract manufacturing + mass consumer advertising + pharmacy shelf.** PharmaSGP (IPO prospectus 2020 [A]): all production outsourced to 36 German contract makers; all sales via pharmacies; marketing €27.8m = **44 % of €62.6m revenue** (2019; my math), mainly print ads aimed at the elderly plus TV; **EBIT €22.4m (36 %)** with **30 staff**. Products that miss break-even within **3–6 months** of launch are dropped. 2024: revenue €118.8m, adj. EBITDA margin 31.3 % (ad-hoc filing [A]).
- **It started small and failed first, but not cheaply.** First product Kijimea (IBS, medical device) launched March 2009 and **flopped** despite a media deal with ProSiebenSat.1; the restart came via a **media-for-revenue deal** (TV time paid from sales) through Fischer's former mentor, then CEO of ProSiebenSat.1. Diet powder Yokebe (market leader Almased named as "Ideengeber") had €20m gross ad spend in 2013, equal to its sales. First exits: Lactostop to Dermapharm (2014, ~€15m revenue) and Naturwohl/Yokebe to Perrigo (2015) (apotheke adhoc, 2015 [B], stale).
- **It is venture-scale: the biggest evidence of "big" in this repo so far.** Forbes estimates SYNformulas at ~$100m revenue (2024) with a 30 % margin and Fischer's fortune at $1bn; PharmaSGP was taken private in 2025 ([Forbes, Sep 2025](https://www.forbes.com.au/news/billionaires/this-pharma-billionaire-is-developing-a-cannabis-painkiller-to-replace-dangerous-opioids/) [B]). Comparable: supplement seller Sunday Natural (Berlin, founded 2013) sold to CVC at an EV of €800m incl. debt ([Börsen-Zeitung, Jan 2024](https://www.boersen-zeitung.de/unternehmen-branchen/cvc-uebernimmt-berliner-start-up-fuer-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-sunday-natural) [B]).
- **What a €10k part-time non-clinician can copy:** the product logic (self-pay, chronic, embarrassing or under-treated complaint; cheapest legal product class; contract manufacturer; test-and-kill in 3–6 months). Starting a capsule supplement is possible from 2,000 units with 8–12 weeks lead time at one EU contract maker ([wonnda/BIOPAK](https://wonnda.com/de/lieferanten/biopak/) [B]); a supplement needs only a notification to BVL, no approval (§ 5 NemV [A]).
- **What cannot be copied as is:** (1) the founders' starting position (physician + Novartis executive + HBS MBA + lawyer/BCG, with a mentor at a TV group); (2) the marketing budget (tens of millions); (3) two of the regulatory routes have since become harder: gut products that work by substances are now class IIb/III medical devices under MDR Rule 21 (needs a notified body), and the "chemical-free" OTC drugs are **homeopathic remedies** that ride on a special approval route and carry efficacy and reputation risk (prospectus [A]). *Inference:* with €10k, the only open routes are food supplements, cosmetics and class I devices, sold online first.
- **Exception worth noting:** Kijimea's flagship has a real RCT: 443 IBS patients, 34 % vs 19 % responders (RR 1.7, p = 0.0007), funded by Synformulas ([Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32277872/) [A]). Evidence is a marketing asset in this model ("largest OTC IBS study" is on every product page).

### 5. The pattern (why it works without a payer contract)
1. **Pick a chronic, self-treated, often embarrassing complaint with weak standard care** (IBS, nerve pain, rheumatic pain, sexual weakness, vertigo, lactose intolerance, overweight). Prospectus: PharmaSGP "constantly analyzes its target markets to identify chronic indications with strong untapped demand" [A].
2. **Use the cheapest legal product class that allows a disease-related claim** (homeopathic registration, medical device under the old MDD, supplements for non-disease claims). Keep a pipeline: 38 unused marketing authorisations in 2020 [A].
3. **Outsource everything physical**: contract manufacturers and one logistics provider per country ("asset-light") [A].
4. **Buy demand directly from consumers**: print ads in magazines read by older people, TV at prime slots; full budget from day one; pharmacists' recommendations add up to 30 % of units for some brands [A].
5. **Kill fast**: no break-even within 3–6 months → product dropped [A].
6. **Brand families**: one brand, many products (Kijimea: >20 SKUs) [B].
7. **Exit or roll up**: sell single brands to strategics (Dermapharm, Perrigo), buy brands from big pharma (GSK) [A/B].

Why it works without a payer: German consumers pay out of pocket for health (private households paid €65bn of €538.2bn health spending in 2024; [Ärzteblatt on Destatis, Apr 2026](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/themen/gesundheitskosten/gesundheitsausgaben-betrugen-weit-uber-500-milliarden-euro-00682bf3-5b29-4b7e-82b7-90d7344d9e76) [B]), and self-medication in pharmacies alone was ~€10.2bn in 2024 (IQVIA, see `research/consumer-health-scan.md`).

### 6. What changed since 2009 (headwinds for a copycat)
| Change | Effect | Source |
|---|---|---|
| MDR Rule 21 (substance-based devices) | Products "introduced into the human body via a body orifice" are class III if systemically absorbed, class IIa on skin or in nose/mouth, **class IIb in all other cases**: a Kijimea-type gut device now needs a notified body | Rule text quoted in [AESGP position paper, Mar 2020](https://aesgp.eu/content/uploads/2020/06/AESGP_Position-Paper-Rule-21_March-2020.pdf) [B] (EUR-Lex not reachable from this session) |
| Homeopathy | PharmaSGP's own risk factor: "general discussions on, and skepticism with respect to, the efficacy of such remedies … could adversely affect demand" | prospectus [A] |
| Print and linear TV decline | Prospectus risk factor: print ads "may … become less effective as a result of declining demand for printed publications" | prospectus [A] |
| Online pharmacies grow | OTC mail-order €3.57bn in 2024 (+10.7 %) | IQVIA Q4 2024 [B] |
| Media-for-equity still exists | ProSiebenSat.1's SevenVentures gives TV time for equity or revenue share; Pitch Day 2025 prize €3m media; a menopause startup (Evela Health) reached the semi-final | [ProSiebenSat.1, 22 Oct 2025](https://www.prosiebensat1.com/en/newsroom/edurino-wins-sevenventures-pitch-day2025-597110) [B]; Greenforce (Munich) media-for-equity, seven figures ([Munich Startup, Mar 2025](https://munich-startup.de/news/greenforce-media-for-equity-deal) [B]) |

### 7. Copyability for this founder (founder-brief.md: non-clinician, part-time to Sep 2027, €10k)
| Element | FUTRUE had | Copyable with €10k? | Note (*inference* unless sourced) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indication picking | Physician founder + market data | **Yes** | Desk research can do this (Part 2 scan). Needs a pharmacist/physician advisor for claims |
| Product class | Homeopathic drugs, MDD devices, supplements | **Partly** | Supplements (BVL notification, § 5 NemV [A]), cosmetics, class I devices. Class IIa+ devices and drugs: no |
| Manufacturing | 36 contract makers | **Yes** | MOQ 2,000 units, 8–12 weeks at one EU maker [B]; first-batch cost not sourced from a primary source `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| Clinical evidence | Own RCT (443 patients) | **No** (early) | Use ingredients with published evidence; own trial later |
| Demand generation | Tens of millions in print/TV; media-for-revenue | **No** at that scale | Online (search, social, Amazon) and media-for-equity after traction. CAC not sourced yet (Part 2) |
| Pharmacy channel | 40-person field force, pharmacist conferences | **Later** | Start online (own shop, marketplaces, online pharmacies) |
| Kill discipline | 3–6 months to break-even | **Yes** | Cheapest test: small batches, ad tests before production |
| Exit path | Strategics (Dermapharm, Perrigo), PE (CVC for Sunday Natural) | **Yes, in principle** | Exits shown in [B] sources |
| Grants | None named | *Weak* | Start?Zuschuss! is for digital tech startups and EXIST needs an innovative project with a university host; only a digital part could qualify (*inference*; see `research/consumer-health-scan.md` §5) |

**Co-founder need (inference):** a pharmacist or physician who owns claims and product safety (HWG, Health Claims Regulation), and a performance marketer. The CEO's AI/automation skills fit customer service, advice bots and marketing operations (Kijimea runs free phone advice and a "digital product adviser" on its shop [B]).

**Fit with the mission (inference):** "contribute to tackle future healthcare problems" is met better by products with evidence (Kijimea-type RCT) than by homeopathy. The CEO should decide whether homeopathy and weakly evidenced supplements are no-go (open question in `progress.md`).
