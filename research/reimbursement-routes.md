# Reimbursement & funding routes for digital health products in DE (status late 2026)
Task: A9 (updated by A15: § 113e route, § 8 Abs. 8 uptake; A16: fact-check fixes) | Date: 2026-11-06, updated 2026-11-07 | Status: draft

## Findings
- **DiGA (app on prescription) is still open but is getting tighter on price.** The GKV-BStabG (in force 30 Jul 2026) adds volume discounts from 1 Jan 2027: "mindestens zwei Prozent bei mehr als dreitausend Abgaben … und mindestens dreißig Prozent bei mehr als hunderttausend Abgaben" per year. It also makes group price caps (Höchstbeträge) mandatory for the free-pricing first year, with a weighted-average cap for DiGA in no group. The ≥20 % performance-based price share has applied since 1 Jan 2026 [A] ([§ 134 SGB V, BStabG version](https://www.buzer.de/134_SGB_V.htm); [BStabG cabinet draft, reasoning p. 156–157](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/G/GKV-Beitragssatzstabilisierungsgesetz_Kabinett.pdf)).
  - Size of the market: 58 DiGA were listed at end-2025. GKV spent €171m on DiGA in 2025, or 0.05 % of GKV spending [B] ([Bitkom DiGA study, Oct 2026](https://www.bitkom.org/sites/main/files/2026-10/bitkom-studienbericht-digitale-gesundheitsanwendungen-diga-2026.pdf)).
  - Manufacturers' view: 94 % of 34 surveyed manufacturers rate the cost of producing evidence as high [B] (same source).
- **DiPA (care app on the care insurance) is a route in law only.** "Bisher ist keine DiPA in diesem Verzeichnis gelistet" (BMG draft, 19 Jun 2026) [A] ([DiPAV amendment draft](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/D/1_DiPAV-AendVO_RefE_final.pdf)).
  - The benefit is €53/month in total for the app plus support services [A] ([§ 30 SGB XI notice 2025](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb11_30bek_2025/BJNR634700024.html)).
  - The law excludes apps "zur Arbeitsorganisation von ambulanten Pflegeeinrichtungen, zur Wissensvermittlung, Information oder Kommunikation, zur Beantragung oder Verwaltung von Leistungen" and apps "die ausschließlich auf Auskunft oder Beratung zur Auswahl und Inanspruchnahme von Sozialleistungen … ausgerichtet sind" [A] ([§ 40a Abs. 1a SGB XI](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_11/__40a.html), re-read 2026-11-07). So DiPA cannot pay for a navigation-*only* product (pain-patients P1). *Inference:* a hybrid app whose main purpose is relieving carers or stabilising home care (which Abs. 1a explicitly allows) is not excluded just because it also contains benefits information.
- **PNOG care-digitisation fund: bigger and broader than in the June draft, but no money before H2 2027.** The cabinet draft (30 Sep 2026) sets up €1.6bn from the federal special fund for 2027–2031, plus €400m co-funding from the Länder (80/20). It now covers "ambulanter und stationärer Pflegeeinrichtungen" (the June draft covered only outpatient and day care).
  - Payouts run via the Pflegekassen "ab dem 1. Juli 2027". Per-facility caps and conditions (incl. "Nachweis der Wirtschaftlichkeit", evaluation duty, no double funding) come in GKV-SV guidelines due 30 Jun 2027 [A] ([PNOG cabinet draft, § 11 Abs. 4–9 SGB XI-new, p. 26–28](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/P/Kabinett-GE_Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_PNOG.pdf)).
  - Status: the 1st reading took place on 5 Nov 2026 (bill Drucksache 21/8869); the bill went to the Health Committee, and coalition speakers announced changes, mainly for family carers [B] ([Bundestag text archive, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw45-de-pflegeneuordnungsgesetz-1225076)). Planned: 2nd/3rd reading 11 Dec 2026, in force 1 Jan 2027 [B] ([AOK PNOG tracker, updated 27 Oct 2026](https://www.aok.de/pp/gesetz/pnog/)).
- **New route for nursing homes from 2028: § 113e "Transformationsstellenanteile" (bill).** From 1 Jan 2028 to 31 Dec 2032, a residential home may agree with the Pflegekassen and the social-welfare office, in its care-rate agreement (Pflegesatzvereinbarung), to leave up to 10 % of the posts in its staffing corridor unfilled. It then receives their money value to pay for staff-relief tech. The value is capped at the care minimum wage × 1,560 h per FTE. Only systems on a catalogue with proven relief effect qualify; the Kompetenzzentrum Digitalisierung und Pflege (at the GKV-SV) publishes it from 30 Sep 2027 [A] ([PNOG cabinet draft, art. 1 no. 73, p. 59–60; reasoning p. 232–234](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/P/Kabinett-GE_Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_PNOG.pdf); text unchanged in [Bundestag bill 21/8869](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/088/2108869.pdf)).
  - Unlike the grants, this is **recurring opex money inside the care rate** for up to 5 years. But the payers (GKV-SV) want it replaced by a study until 2031, and nurses' groups (DBfK, ver.di) want it struck [B] (see §4b).
  - *My math (inference):* about €16,000–19,000 per year for a 100-resident home in a realistic case (0.56 FTE); the theoretical ceiling is about €100,000. See §4b for the inputs.
- **The existing grant is used by most facilities, but only about 37 % of the money has been drawn.** The grant (§ 8 Abs. 8 SGB XI: 40 % of spend, max €12,000 per facility, since 2019) paid out **€143.3m nationally in 2019–2024**: €5.2m (2019), €26.6m, €31.6m, €27.6m, €28.1m, and €24.2m in 2024 [A] ([BAS, Finanzierungsanteile 2019–2022](https://www.bundesamtsozialesicherung.de/fileadmin/redaktion/Pflegeversicherung/Ausgleichsfonds/Finanzierungsanteile_GKV_PKV_2023.pdf); [BAS, 2023–2024](https://www.bundesamtsozialesicherung.de/fileadmin/redaktion/Ausgleichfonds/20250320Finanzierungsanteile_PKV_GKV_ab_2023.pdf)).
  - DAK processes applications for 48 % of facilities: "rund 11.300 von den etwa 17.500" of them had used the grant by mid-2025, but only €81.2m ("knapp 40 Prozent") of the money was drawn [B] ([DAK, 1 Sep 2025](https://www.dak.de/presse/bundesthemen/politik-unternehmensnachrichten/digitalisierung-70-prozent-der-pflegeeinrichtungen-fehlt-anbindung-an-telematikinfrastruktur_146652)).
  - vdek's "ca. 30 Prozent" (Berlin) and "ca. 20 Prozent" (Brandenburg) count facilities that used up the **full** amount, not facilities that used it at all [B] ([vdek Berlin 2024](https://www.vdek.com/LVen/BERBRA/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/Berlin/2024/Digitalisierung-in-der-Pflege.html); [vdek Brandenburg 2024](https://www.vdek.com/LVen/BERBRA/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/Brandenburg/2024/Digitalisierung-in-der-Pflege.html)).
  - *My math:* 143.3 / (32,054 facilities × €0.012m = €384.6m) = 37 % of the theoretical pool by end-2024; 11,300 / 17,500 = 65 % of DAK-processed facilities. The typical draw was ~€7,200 (81.2m / 11,300), i.e. ~€18,000 spend at 40 %. Grant money is not the same as purchases; the payouts have been flat at €24–32m a year since 2020.
- **Hospitals have no new software budget, but they do have a stick.**
  - The €29bn transformation fund pays for structural projects that the Länder apply for (concentration, closures, telemedicine networks, emergency structures). Digital infrastructure is eligible only as part of such projects, and "Vorhaben, die überwiegend dem Erhalt bestehender Strukturen dienen, sind nicht förderfähig" [A] ([§ 12b KHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/khg/__12b.html)).
  - The KHZG money (€3bn federal; up to €4.3bn with Länder co-funding) is committed, and no successor programme was found in my searches.
  - The KHZG penalty (up to 2 % per case) starts weighting **actual use** of five digital services: 20 % of the penalty in 2027, rising to 80 % in 2031. Use above 60 % (2027) to 80 % (2029+) avoids the use part [A] ([GKV-SV/DKG Digitalisierungsabschlags-Vereinbarung](https://www.dkgev.de/fileadmin/Mediapool/2_Themen/2.1_Digitalisierung_Daten/2.1.5._Telematik-Infrastruktur/2.1.5.1_Finanzierungsvereinbarung/2026-01-26_ANLAGE_3_DIGITALISIERUNGSABSCHLAGSVEREINBARUNG.pdf)).
- **Insurer budgets are being squeezed by law.**
  - Selective contracts (§ 140a) must now meet the contribution-stability principle (§ 71) [A] ([BStabG art. 1 no. 57](https://www.buzer.de/gesetz/17632/a340690.htm)).
  - Insurers' admin costs per insured person may grow only with the Grundlohnrate from 2027 [A] ([BStabG reasoning on § 4](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/G/GKV-Beitragssatzstabilisierungsgesetz_Kabinett.pdf)).
  - The Innovationsfonds falls permanently to €100m/yr from 2027 [A] ([G-BA, 10 Aug 2026](https://innovationsfonds.g-ba.de/downloads/media/397/2026-08-10_Ueberblick-fuer-Antragstellende-ueber-Foerderbekanntmachungen-2026-und-Verfahrensarten.pdf)).
- **Inference (not a source finding):** for a part-time founder with a small team, the most realistic first revenue is **B2B sales to providers**, with the provider's own budget as the base. Three triggers help:
  - care facilities from H2 2027, when they can co-finance through the PNOG fund;
  - residential homes from 2028, via § 113e, if the bill passes unchanged and the product is on the catalogue (catalogue entry needs relief evidence first);
  - hospitals now, under the 2027+ KHZG usage penalty.

  DiGA, DiPA, selective contracts and Innovationsfonds are slow or evidence-heavy routes. They suit a second product, not the first.

### 2. DiGA: what changed in 2026
- **BStabG (24 Jul 2026, BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 228, in force 30 Jul 2026)** added three changes to § 134 SGB V [A] ([buzer § 134](https://www.buzer.de/134_SGB_V.htm)):
  - **Volume discount from 2027.** The reasoning sets a scale of 2 % (3,001–9,999 uses/yr), 3 %, 5 %, 10 %, 20 % (50,000–99,999) and 30 % (≥100,000) [A] ([BStabG Kabinett, p. 156](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/G/GKV-Beitragssatzstabilisierungsgesetz_Kabinett.pdf)).
  - **Group caps on the first-year price are now mandatory,** with a fallback cap: the use-weighted mean of the group caps. This is meant to make first-year pricing more uniform (same source).
  - **Unchanged:** the free manufacturer price in year 1, the ≥20 % performance-based share (since 1 Jan 2026), and the 9-month negotiation window plus 3-month arbitration (§ 134 Abs. 2) [A] ([gesetze-im-internet § 134](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_5/__134.html)).
- **Evidence path** [A] ([§ 139e SGB V](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_5/__139e.html)):
  - BfArM decides within 3 months (+3 in justified cases).
  - Trial listing ("Erprobung", ≤12 months) is only for "niedriger Risikoklasse" (I/IIa) and needs an evaluation concept written by a party independent of the manufacturer.
  - BfArM publishes the results of in-use outcome measurement from 1 Jan 2026. Bitkom says this measurement has been mandatory since Jul 2026 [B].
- **GeDIG (cabinet 15 Jul 2026; Bundestag 1st reading 8 Oct 2026; not yet passed)** [A] ([GeDIG cabinet draft](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/G/GeDIG_Kabinett.pdf), [BMG GeDIG page](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/service/gesetze-und-verordnungen/detail/gedig)):
  - clarifies that DiGA include remote monitoring ("Fernüberwachung des Gesundheitszustands");
  - replaces the GKV-SV DiGA report with raw-data reporting to the BMG;
  - lets DiGA receive ePA data with patient consent.
- **HiB draft (3 Nov 2026):** procedural only for DiGA (moves arbitration rules to a new § 71d) [A] ([HiB RefE, p. 50 and 200](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/H/RefEntwurf_Hilfsmittelmodernisierungs-_und_Buerokratierueckbaugesetz_HiB.pdf)).
- **Market data:**
  - 74 DiGA ever listed; 58 at end-2025 (48 permanent, 10 on trial); 32 of them for mental health.
  - About 1.9m prescriptions/approvals; 1.6m codes activated; 695,000 activations in 2025 (+63 %).
  - 87 % of activations come from prescriptions; GPs write 51 % of prescriptions [B] ([Bitkom 2026](https://www.bitkom.org/sites/main/files/2026-10/bitkom-studienbericht-digitale-gesundheitsanwendungen-diga-2026.pdf)).
- **Payer view:**
  - "Nur weniger als jede fünfte DiGA konnte mit ihrer Aufnahme einen Nutzen nachweisen"; 16 were delisted.
  - €63m was pre-financed; prices range "von 119 Euro bis zu 2.077 Euro".
  - The payers welcome the FinanzKommission's proposals to change trial listing and pricing [B] (payer-association press release) ([GKV-SV PM 8 Apr 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/gkv_spitzenverband/presse/pressemitteilungen_und_statements/pressemitteilung_2239872.jsp)).
- **Manufacturer view** (Bitkom, n=34) [B]:
  - 74 % see pay as inadequate relative to effort;
  - 85 % cite high development and approval costs;
  - 100 % say the general situation got worse over the past two years (82 % "stark verschlechtert", 18 % "eher verschlechtert");
  - yet 50 % plan more DiGA.
- *Inference:* for a first product, DiGA means 12–24+ months of evidence and certification work and a CE-marked medical device (MDR). That is outside the founder's sweet spot (`founder-brief.md`) unless a clinical/regulatory co-founder joins. The FinanzKommission's second report (due end-2026, see `research/trends.md`) may tighten things further.

### 3. DiPA
- Legal frame [A] ([§ 40a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_11/__40a.html), [§ 40b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_11/__40b.html), [§ 78a SGB XI](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_11/__78a.html)):
  - Eligible: apps for the person in need of care, and since then also apps that support family carers ("pflegende Angehörige … entlasten").
  - Excluded (§ 40a Abs. 1a): work organisation of *outpatient* care services, knowledge transfer/information/communication, applying for or managing benefits, and apps "ausschließlich" for information or advice on choosing social benefits. Carer-relief apps are explicitly included.
  - Medical devices must be low risk class. The price is agreed within 3 months of listing.
- €53/month for app plus support services together (from 1 Jan 2025) [A] ([§ 30 notice](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb11_30bek_2025/BJNR634700024.html)).
- Zero listed, and few applications [A] ([DiPAV draft, 19 Jun 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/D/1_DiPAV-AendVO_RefE_final.pdf)). The draft brings in:
  - trial listing (Erprobung) with an evaluation concept;
  - a care benefit that also counts if family carers are relieved.
- PNOG only renumbers the DiPA rules (old § 39a becomes § 40c) and changes nothing of substance [A] (PNOG cabinet draft, art. 8 reasoning, p. 247).
- *Inference:* a carer-relief app (pain-patients P3) could in principle become the first DiPA. But €53/month is shared with support services, there is no track record, and BfArM procedures apply. This is a high-risk route; treat it as an option, not a plan.

### 4. PNOG digital fund (care facilities)
- **Text (cabinet draft, 30 Sep 2026)** [A] ([PNOG Kabinett, p. 26–28, reasoning p. 187–188](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/P/Kabinett-GE_Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_PNOG.pdf)):
  - "Förderprogramm für die Digitalisierung ambulanter und stationärer Pflegeeinrichtungen in Höhe von 1,6 Milliarden Euro", 2027–2031, paid in five equal yearly tranches to the care insurance equalisation fund at the BAS.
  - **The Länder "sollen" co-fund** €400m in total (Königsteiner key). The federal tranche for a Land is transferred only if that Land secures its share in its budget (by 30 Jun in 2027, by 30 Nov in later years) and keeps its existing investment funding at least at its 2024–2026 average. So the €2.0bn total holds only if every Land takes part (reasoning p. 187).
  - **Eligible measures:** (1) care and documentation systems, plus administration, organisation and management systems; (2) assistive systems; (3) IT infrastructure; (4) staff training; (5) interoperable interfaces; (6) IT security.
  - Facilities apply to the Pflegekasse in charge. The GKV-SV guideline (by **30 Jun 2027**, with PKV, BAS and the Länder) sets the maximum per facility, the procedure, proof of economic and sustainable use, and evaluation duties. Misused funds or refusing the evaluation lead to repayment.
- **Change vs the June draft:** residential homes (stationär) are now in (the June draft excluded them and pointed them to § 113e; GKV-SV objected [B], [GKV-SV statement 10 Jun 2026, p. on Nr. 73](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/Stellungnahmen_WP21/Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_-_PNOG/GKV-SV_Stellungnahmen_PNOG.pdf)). `research/trends.md` was updated in A10.
  - **Inconsistency in the bill:** the law text of § 11 Abs. 4 says "ambulanter und stationärer Pflegeeinrichtungen", but the general reasoning in both the cabinet draft and bill 21/8869 still says "ambulanten und teilstationären Einrichtungen" [A] (bill 21/8869, general part). The law text governs, but the committee may still settle scope either way.
  - **The old grant narrows from 2027:** in PNOG § 11 Abs. 3 (old § 8 Abs. 8) the purpose "zur Entlastung der Pflegekräfte" and the list of workflow items ("verbesserte Arbeitsabläufe und Organisation", quality management) are dropped. Instead it names care, activation and social participation of residents, plus training incl. "sachgerechten Umgang mit künstlicher Intelligenz". The reasoning says this avoids double funding with the new fund (Abs. 4). New GKV-SV guidelines are due by 31 Mar 2027 [A] (PNOG cabinet draft p. 25–26, reasoning p. 186; [current § 8 Abs. 8](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_11/__8.html)). *Inference:* from 2027, staff-workflow software would be funded from the new fund, not the €12k grant.
- **Size per facility (my math, inference):** facilities in 2023: 16,505 nursing homes + 15,549 outpatient services = 32,054 [A] ([Destatis table](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Gesundheit/Pflege/Tabellen/pflegeeinrichtungen-deutschland.html)).
  - €2.0bn ÷ 32,054 ≈ **€62,400 per facility over 5 years** (≈ €12,500/yr).
  - Federal share only: €1.6bn ÷ 32,054 ≈ €49,900.
  - The real cap depends on the guideline and on the Länder co-funding.
- **Timetable (planned)** [B] ([AOK tracker](https://www.aok.de/pp/gesetz/pnog/)):
  - Bundestag: 1st reading 5 Nov 2026, hearing 11 or 25 Nov, 2nd/3rd reading 11 Dec 2026.
  - Bundesrat: 1st pass 20 Nov, 2nd pass 18 Dec 2026.
  - In force 1 Jan 2027 / 1 Jan 2028. A structural care reform is expected in a separate law (cabinet by Easter 2027).
- **Precedent uptake (critic issue 3 in `research/reviews/2026-11-06-trends.md`; fact-check fix 1 in `research/reviews/2026-11-07-reimbursement-routes.md`):**
  - The § 8 Abs. 8 grant (40 %, max €12,000) has run since 2019, now to 2030.
  - **National payouts (official) [A]:** the BAS lists the yearly total cost of "Förderung digitaler Anwendungen in Pflegeeinrichtungen" (the base for the 7 % PKV share):

    | Year | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | Sum |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | € | 5,187,883 | 26,639,280 | 31,641,717 | 27,590,391 | 28,072,975 | 24,164,292 | 143,296,538 |

    Sources: [BAS 2019–2022](https://www.bundesamtsozialesicherung.de/fileadmin/redaktion/Pflegeversicherung/Ausgleichsfonds/Finanzierungsanteile_GKV_PKV_2023.pdf), [BAS 2023–2024](https://www.bundesamtsozialesicherung.de/fileadmin/redaktion/Ausgleichfonds/20250320Finanzierungsanteile_PKV_GKV_ab_2023.pdf). Sum is my addition.
  - **Facility share (DAK, 48 % of facilities) [B]:** ~11,300 of ~17,500 used it by mid-2025 (+~12 % vs a year earlier); ~21,000 applications, 93 % approved; €81.2m drawn = "knapp 40 Prozent" ([DAK, 1 Sep 2025](https://www.dak.de/presse/bundesthemen/politik-unternehmensnachrichten/digitalisierung-70-prozent-der-pflegeeinrichtungen-fehlt-anbindung-an-telematikinfrastruktur_146652)). Most other facilities are processed by the AOK.
  - **Full-amount users (vdek, regional) [B]:** Berlin ~30 % and Brandenburg ~20 % had used the full sum by 2024; Hessen "noch zu wenig genutzt"; Hamburg had paid "mehr als 826.300 Euro" by 2021 (vdek releases in Sources; Hamburg figure >3 years old).
  - *My math (inference):* pool = 32,054 facilities (2023) × €12,000 = €384.6m; €143.3m / €384.6m = **37 % drawn by end-2024**, which matches DAK's "knapp 40 Prozent". 11,300 / 17,500 = **65 % of DAK-processed facilities used it at least once**. €81.2m / 11,300 ≈ €7,200 per using facility. In 2024, €24.2m of grants at 40 % implies at least ~€60m of grant-backed facility spend (24.2 / 0.4).
  - **Reading:** most facilities use the grant, but they draw on average ~60 % of the cap, and yearly payouts are flat, not rising. No AOK or GKV-SV facility count was found (GKV-SV's Aug 2026 position paper gives no uptake figure; it asks for a grant every three years instead of once [B], [GKV-SV position paper, 27 Aug 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/service_1/publikationen/20260827_PoPa_DigitalisierunginderPflege_barrierefrei.pdf)).
  - Bavaria adds 40 %/€12,000 from a €15.0m budget [A] ([BayernPortal, 26 Apr 2026](https://www.bayernportal.de/dokumente/leistung/3508639933297)).
  - A 2024 survey of 694 Bavarian care facilities (cited in a Landtag motion): "Nur 3,5 Prozent der Einrichtungen geben an, an die TI angebunden zu sein" [B] ([Bayerischer Landtag Drs. 19/11932, 6 May 2026](https://www.bayern.landtag.de/www/ElanTextAblage_WP19/Drucksachen/Basisdrucksachen/0000009000/0000009339.pdf)) (survey from 2024; may be outdated).
- *Inference:* the fund turns a care facility's software purchase into a 20–60 % own-share decision. Why facilities draw only ~60 % of the old cap is **not known from sources**: DAK itself says "Es muss nun analysiert werden, weshalb die Abrufquote weiterhin eher niedrig ist" and asks for simpler access, and the GKV-SV asks that funding options be made better known. IT capacity is one possible reason, not an established one. If it holds, a startup that **does the grant paperwork and implementation** for the facility could unlock more demand than one that only sells software (hypothesis for interviews). Earliest grant-backed revenue: Q3–Q4 2027.

### 4b. § 113e Transformationsstellenanteile (residential homes, bill)
- **Text** (PNOG art. 1 no. 73; identical in bill 21/8869) [A] ([cabinet draft p. 59–60, reasoning p. 232–234](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/GuV/P/Kabinett-GE_Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_PNOG.pdf); [bill 21/8869](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/088/2108869.pdf)):
  - **Who:** only "vollstationäre Pflegeeinrichtungen". Not outpatient services, not day care.
  - **What:** for the corridor between the minimum staffing in the Land framework contract and the maximum under [§ 113c Abs. 1 Nr. 2 and 3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_11/__113c.html) (assistants with ≥1 year training, and registered nurses), the parties "können … für bis zu 10 Prozent dieser Stellenanteile Transformationsstellenanteile in der Pflegesatzvereinbarung vereinbaren". These count as equal to staff. The money may be used "ausschließlich" for staff-supporting or relieving technical/digital systems "die in dem Katalog nach Absatz 5 enthalten sind". The ban on investment costs in care rates (§ 82 Abs. 2) does not apply here.
  - **Amount:** at most the minimum wage under the PflegeArbbV × 1,560 net hours per FTE (reasoning: "vergleichsweise niedrigere Aufwendungen … da beispielsweise keine Sozialversicherungsbeiträge"). Systems funded elsewhere are excluded (no double funding).
  - **When:** 1 Jan 2028 to 31 Dec 2032. Catalogue from 30 Sep 2027, updated quarterly. Evaluation 30 Jun 2030 and 30 Jun 2033. First GKV-SV report to the BMG on 1 Jul 2028.
  - **Evidence:** catalogue entry needs a relief effect "auf Grundlage wissenschaftlicher Erkenntnisse oder systematischer Praxisevaluationen nachgewiesen". In the negotiation the home must state which solution it will fund and what effect it expects, based on "eine grobe Analyse der internen Arbeitsprozesse". Systems must not harm care quality, self-determination or safety (Abs. 6).
  - **Who pays:** the care rate. Per the GKV-SV, in residential care this means "steigende Pflegesätze bzw. Eigenanteile und damit … die Pflegebedürftigen" [B] (GKV-SV statement, see below).
- **Minimum wage inputs** [A]/[B]: 7th PflegeArbbV, from 1 Jul 2026: €16.52 / €17.80 / €21.03 per hour (assistant / qualified assistant / registered nurse); from 1 Jul 2027: €16.95 / €18.26 / €21.58; valid to 30 Sep 2028 ([Bundesregierung](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/mindestlohn-altenpflege-steigt-2216632); [BMG, 25 Nov 2025](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/bmg-bmas-pflegemindestlohn-25-11-25)). Rates after Sep 2028 are not set.
- **Size per home (my math, inference; the corridor size is the big unknown):**
  - Value per FTE in 2028 = 1,560 h × €18.26 = **€28,486** (qualified assistant) or × €21.58 = **€33,665** (nurse).
  - *Realistic case:* the IT trade body FINSOZ puts the corridor for a 100-resident home at ~5.6 FTE (40 % of a 14-FTE extra need found by the PeBeM staffing study) [B] ([FINSOZ statement on the PNOG draft](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/Stellungnahmen_WP21/Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_-_PNOG/FINSOZ_Stellungnahme_PNOG.pdf)). 10 % × 5.6 = 0.56 FTE → **€15,950–18,850 per year**.
  - *Ceiling:* the nurses' association DBfK applies 10 % to all qualified-staff posts of an 80-resident home (25.46 FTE → 2.55 FTE) [B] ([DBfK chart, 31 Aug 2026](https://www.dbfk.de/media/img/regionalverbaende/rvnw/aktuelles/dbfk_schaubild_pnog_personalwirkung-stationaer-2026-08-31.pdf)). Valued: 0.884 FTE × €28,486 + 1.663 FTE × €33,665 = **€81,170/yr** (≈ €101,000 per 100 residents). My reading of Abs. 2 ("10 Prozent dieser Stellenanteile" = posts in the corridor) is that this is an upper bound that assumes a minimum of zero.
  - *National (inference):* 800,000 residents in full residential care (Dec 2023) [A] ([Destatis via Presseportal](https://www.presseportal.de/pm/32102/5933248)). Realistic case for all homes: 4,480 FTE × €28,486–33,665 = **€128–151m/yr**. If only the ~one third of homes with staffing agreed by qualification group can use it (see GKV-SV below): **~€43–50m/yr**. For comparison, the § 8 Abs. 8 grant pays €24–32m/yr. The DBfK-style ceiling (~€810m/yr) is not a realistic market.
- **Positions (all [B], interest groups):**
  - **GKV-SV (the payer side that negotiates the care rates), 10 Jun 2026:** "Digitale Technologien dürfen nicht pauschal als Ersatz für nicht besetzte Personalstellen angesetzt werden." It says only about one third of residential homes have a staffing agreement split by qualification group, which the corridor needs. It also says homes with minimum staffing get the largest claims (a perverse incentive), and that payers would need a new proof procedure, with clawbacks under § 115 Abs. 3. It proposes replacing § 113e with a catalogue and evaluation by 30 Sep 2031, and requiring a "Wirtschaftlichkeitsnachweis mit bezifferbaren Effizienzgewinnen" for listing ([GKV-SV statement](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/Stellungnahmen_WP21/Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_-_PNOG/GKV-SV_Stellungnahmen_PNOG.pdf)). The same line is in its Aug 2026 position paper.
  - **DBfK (nurses):** "§ 113e SGB XI ist zu streichen oder grundlegend zu überarbeiten" ([DBfK statement, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.dbfk.de/media/docs/Berufspolitik/stellungnahmen/2026-06-10_DBfK_Stellungnahme-PNOG.pdf); repeated 7 Sep 2026 in [DBfK top changes](https://www.dbfk.de/media/docs/regionalverbaende/rvnw/aktuelles/2026-09-07_DBfK_TOP-Aenderungsbedarfe-PNOG.pdf)).
  - **ver.di (union):** criticises it "in dreifacher Hinsicht": less pressure to fill posts, no co-determination guarantees, and no scientific proof that digital support improves working conditions ([ver.di statement](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/Gesetze_und_Verordnungen/Stellungnahmen_WP21/Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz_-_PNOG/verdi_Stellungnahme_PNOG.pdf)).
  - **FINSOZ (social-sector IT vendors):** supports it and asks for 50 % instead of 10 %, wage at the regional level instead of the minimum wage, and evaluation during use rather than before catalogue entry (as with DiPA trial listing) (FINSOZ statement above).
- **What it means (inference):**
  - Of all routes in this file, this is the only recurring, non-grant money tied to **staff relief in nursing homes**. That fits the founder's sweet spot (workflow/automation). But it pays only for catalogue-listed products. So a 2027 pilot with a **systematic practice evaluation** (time saved per shift) is the entry ticket, and it has to be ready around the first catalogue (30 Sep 2027).
  - Risk is high: the payer side and nurses oppose it, the bill is in committee, and the money comes from residents' co-pays, which are already a political issue (€3,364/month in year 1, see `research/trends.md`). Treat it as an upside, not as the base case for the first revenue.
  - The catalogue criteria (what counts as a "systematische Praxisevaluation") are not defined yet. They are the key unknown, together with whether the Bundestag keeps § 113e.

### 5. Hospitals: transformation fund and KHZG follow-up
- **Transformation fund** [A] ([§ 12b KHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/khg/__12b.html)):
  - €29bn from 2026 to 2035; the Länder apply (up to 95 % of their Königsteiner share).
  - Eligible: cross-site concentration "einschließlich der erforderlichen Angleichung der digitalen Infrastruktur", sector-bridging care units, "telemedizinischer Netzwerkstrukturen", centres, hospital networks, integrated emergency structures, closures, training capacity.
  - *Inference:* a startup can only be a subcontractor inside a hospital's structural project. This is not a direct route.
- **KHZG:** €3bn federal fund, with ≥30 % co-funding by the Land and/or operator [A] ([§ 14a KHG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/khg/__14a.html)). Applications closed in 2021 (§ 14a). No successor programme was found. The DKG demands operating-cost funding: "Ohne verlässliche Regelungen zur Betriebskostenfinanzierung können wir das erreichte Niveau nicht dauerhaft halten" [B] ([DKG PM 17 Jun 2026](https://www.dkgev.de/fileadmin/Mediapool/1_DKG/1.7_Presse/1.7.1_Pressemitteilungen/2026/2026-06-17_PM_DKG_zum_Digitalradar.docx)).
  - The DigitalRadar maturity score rose from 33.3 (2021) to 55.0 (2026) [B] (same source). This resolves a `[UNVERIFIED]` item in `research/trends.md`.
- **Penalty from use** [A] ([Digitalisierungsabschlags-Vereinbarung](https://www.dkgev.de/fileadmin/Mediapool/2_Themen/2.1_Digitalisierung_Daten/2.1.5._Telematik-Infrastruktur/2.1.5.1_Finanzierungsvereinbarung/2026-01-26_ANLAGE_3_DIGITALISIERUNGSABSCHLAGSVEREINBARUNG.pdf); [§ 5 Abs. 3h KHEntgG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/khentgg/__5.html)):
  - Up to 2 % of the bill per case.
  - Availability vs use: 100/0 in 2025–26; 80/20 in 2027 (no use penalty above 60 % use); 60/40 in 2028 (>70 %); 50/50 in 2029; 40/60 in 2030; 20/80 in 2031 (>80 %).
  - Services covered (FTB 2–6, [§ 19 KHSFV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/khsfv/__19.html)):
    - FTB 2: patient portals (digital admission and discharge);
    - FTB 3: structured, also speech-based, nursing and treatment documentation;
    - FTB 4: clinical decision support;
    - FTB 5: digital medication management;
    - FTB 6: digital order entry.
- *Inference:* from 2027 hospitals pay for *unused* software. That creates demand for adoption tools such as speech documentation, discharge and patient-portal workflows, and usage analytics, funded from hospital opex. Budgets are under the 2027–29 price cap (see `research/trends.md`), so ROI has to be shown in months. Clinical decision support is likely an MDR device; avoid it as a first product.

### 6. Insurer routes after BStabG
- **§ 140a selective contracts.** The new sentence "§ 71 Absatz 1 bis 3 gilt" makes the contribution-stability principle mandatory for these contracts [A] ([BStabG art. 1 no. 57](https://www.buzer.de/gesetz/17632/a340690.htm); reasoning p. 157). Partners can be care providers, pharma or device makers (§ 140a Abs. 4a); "digitale Angebote … wie etwa eine App mit besonderen Extras" are possible [B] ([Barmer Innovation-Guide](https://www.barmer.de/leistungserbringer/innovationen-einreichen/innovation-guide-so-kommen-sie-zu-einer-foerderung-1280800)).
- **§ 68a/§ 68b** (insurers fund digital innovations and care innovations; data analysis allowed): unchanged in 2026 [A] ([§ 68a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_5/__68a.html), [§ 68b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_5/__68b.html)). GeDIG would let insurers send information on "individuell geeigneten Versorgungsinnovationen" together with § 25b notices [A] (GeDIG Kabinett, p. 154).
- **Admin-cost cap from 2027:** net admin spend per insured person may rise only by the Grundlohnrate. IT security spending is exempt [A] (BStabG reasoning on § 4, p. 99–100). Relevant to A12 (invoice-processing outsourcing is admin spend).
- **Satzungsleistungen:** Barmer runs a "Budget für digitale Gesundheitsleistungen (DiGL, §28)" [B] (Barmer guide). BStabG only removes homeopathy from statutory-benefit extras [A] (BStabG reasoning on § 11).
- **Prevention (§ 20, ZPP)** [A] ([GKV-SV criteria, 17 Dec 2025](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/krankenversicherung_1/praevention__selbsthilfe__beratung/praevention/praevention_leitfaden/Kriterien_zur_Zertifizierung_digitalen_Praeventionsangebote_gemaess_Kapitel_7_Stand_17.12.2025.pdf)); [B] ([vdek 2026](https://www.vdek.com/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/zentrale-pruefstelle-praevention-mehr-praeventionskurse.html)):
  - Certification requires at least a prospective pre-post study whose design is registered in advance (DRKS/OSF). A 1-year provisional certificate is possible on the basis of the study design.
  - The market: 115,000 certified offers from almost 65,000 providers. "2024 haben rund 1,9 Millionen Versicherte eine von ihrer Krankenkasse geförderte Präventionsmaßnahme genutzt" (any insurer-funded prevention measure, not only ZPP-certified courses; re-read 2026-11-07).
  - *Inference:* crowded and low-price. It suits consumer prevention, not the founder's B2B sweet spot.
- **Innovationsfonds:** €100m/yr from 2027 (down from €200m); startups take part only as consortium partners [A] ([G-BA overview](https://innovationsfonds.g-ba.de/downloads/media/397/2026-08-10_Ueberblick-fuer-Antragstellende-ueber-Foerderbekanntmachungen-2026-und-Verfahrensarten.pdf)); see also `research/munich-ecosystem.md`.
