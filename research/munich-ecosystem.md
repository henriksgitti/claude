# Munich ecosystem: grants, capital, co-founders
Task: A6 | Date: 2026-11-06 | Status: draft

## Findings
- **Part-time status is the binding constraint.** Both founder stipends require leaving the full-time job:
  - **EXIST Gründungsstipendium** (€2,500/month for graduates, 12 months, plus €30,000 material costs for a team) excludes "einem … aktiven Beschäftigungsverhältnis". It allows paid side jobs of at most 20 hours per *month*. A "ruhendes Beschäftigungsverhältnis (Gründungssabbatical)" is allowed. A German Hochschule must apply on the founder's behalf ([EXIST guideline 2023](https://exist.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Richtlinie-EGS-18-04-2023.pdf), [exist.de](https://exist.de/programm/gruendungsstipendium/)) [A].
  - **FLÜGGE** (Bavaria, €2,500/month) is only open to members of a *state Bavarian* Hochschule. In the 2025/1 call it funded up to six projects ([FLÜGGE call 2025/1](https://www.fips.bayern.de/download/foerderaufruf/Foerderaufruf_FLUEGGE_1_2025_final.pdf)) [A].
  - Inference: no stipend is reachable while employed. The realistic path is part-time validation now, then EXIST with a sabbatical or after resigning. The current EXIST guideline accepts applications until 31 Dec 2027.
- **Part-time-compatible support exists, and it is free:**
  - HM's SCE Incubation asks for "mindestens 10 Stunden pro Woche", takes applications on a rolling basis and prepares teams for EXIST ([SCE](https://www.sce.de/incubation.html)) [B].
  - The UnternehmerTUM Incubator lists "Berufstätige" in its target group, focuses on co-founder matching and takes no equity ([UnternehmerTUM](https://www.unternehmertum.de/angebot/incubator)) [B].
  - The Bavarian business plan competition's phase 1 deadline is 13 Jan 2027 (€85,000 prize money) ([BayStartUP](https://www.baystartup.de/businessplan-wettbewerbe)) [B].
  - By contrast, the UnternehmerTUM Accelerator requires "Ihr arbeitet in Vollzeit an eurem Startup" ([UnternehmerTUM](https://www.unternehmertum.de/angebot/accelerator)) [B].
- **First non-dilutive money after incorporation is Start?Zuschuss!.** It covers 50 % of costs, max €36,000, for digital startups at most 2 years old. Round 23 is planned for **25 Nov 2026 – 10 Jan 2027** ([Munich Startup, 16 May 2026](https://www.munich-startup.de/news/start-zuschuss-jetzt-bewerben), [BayernPortal](https://www.bayernportal.de/dokumente/leistung/1868363232175)) [A/B].
  - Order matters: EXIST must be approved *before* founding a company, and *before* Start?Zuschuss! ([Munich Startup](https://www.munich-startup.de/news/start-zuschuss-jetzt-bewerben)).
  - BayDiGuP (Bavarian health ministry) funds digital health and care projects from natural or legal persons at up to 90 %, usually around 50 % for companies, through calls. The guideline expires 31 Dec 2027 ([gesetze-bayern.de](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/BayVV_2126_0_G_14560/true), [StMGP call 2025](https://stmgp.bayern.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/foerderaufruf_digitalisierung_und_ki_in_der_pflege.pdf)) [A].
- **EU money is not for the idea stage:**
  - EIC Accelerator grants (up to €2.5M) need TRL 6–8. The 2027 full-proposal cut-offs are 20 Jan, 17 Mar, 1 Sep and 4 Nov 2027 ([EIC](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en)) [A].
  - EIT Health is headquartered in Munich ([EIT Health contact](https://eithealth.eu/contact-us/)). Its start-up programmes (Catapult, Start-ups Meet Pharma) are currently "Closed for applications" ([EIT Health](https://eithealth.eu/what-we-do/acceleration/)) [B].
  - EIT Jumpstarter "primarily targets" RIS countries (Germany is not one) ([EIT](https://eit.europa.eu/our-activities/opportunities/eit-jumpstarter-2026)) [A].
- **Capital is available, and Munich health deals in 2026 favour B2B workflow AI, which matches the founder's sweet spot:**
  - Bayern was #3 by volume in H1 2026 (>€1.1bn) and #2 by number of rounds (93). Health was among the top sectors with >€450M nationally ([EY Startup-Barometer, Jul 2026](https://www.ey.com/de_de/newsroom/2026/07/ey-startup-barometer-deutschland-juli-2026)) [B].
  - Calliora (AI for hospital billing, Munich) raised >€5.3M pre-seed led by Bertelsmann Healthcare Investments and Redalpine ([Munich Startup, 8 Oct 2026](https://www.munich-startup.de/en/news/munich-ai-startup-calliora-raises-e5-3-million-for-hospital-billing)) [B]. **This is a direct competitor for A11 (hospital billing audits).**
  - Avelios (AI-native hospital information system, HTGF portfolio) took strategic money from SAP and Fresenius ([Munich Startup](https://www.munich-startup.de/en/news/fresenius-invests-in-munich-healthtech-startup-avelios-medical)) [B].
- **Co-founder channels are concrete:**
  - CTO-side:
    - UnternehmerTUM Incubator (above).
    - Munich Co-Founder Matching meetup: 3,182 members, monthly events of about 100 people ([Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/de-de/munich-co-founder-matching/), [Munich Startup](https://www.munich-startup.de/startups-and-ecosystem?tab=ecosystem)) [C/B].
    - TUM.ai, with 150+ students ([TUM.ai](https://www.tum-ai.com/)).
  - Clinical side:
    - TUM Venture Lab Healthcare's 6-month Healthcare Innovation Program, which mixes medicine, informatics and business ([TUM VL](https://www.tum-venture-labs.de/events/healthcare-innovation-program-hip-demo-day/)) [B].
    - The quarterly Healthcare Happy Hour, now in its 10th year ([TUM VL](https://www.tum-venture-labs.de/events/healthcare-happy-hour/)) [B].
  - Antler's Continental Europe residency now runs from Berlin with a launch pad in Amsterdam, not Munich ([Antler](https://www.antler.co/location/germany)) [B].

Demand-side subsidy (relevant for care-sector buyers, not for the startup):
- Bavaria tops up the federal § 8 Abs. 8 SGB XI care-digitisation grant by 40 % of costs, up to €12,000 per facility. The combined maximum is €24,000, and the facility's own share falls from 60 % to 20 %. The Bavarian budget is €15.0M ([BayernPortal, Stand 26.04.2026](https://www.bayernportal.de/dokumente/leistung/3508639933297)) [A]. This feeds the critic's §8(8) point in A10.

Inference (sequence for this founder):
1. Now, part-time: validation and SCE or UnternehmerTUM Incubator, plus the BayStartUP phase 1 deadline (13 Jan 2027).
2. Find a Munich Hochschule host (SCE/HM or TUM/LMU via a co-founder) and apply for EXIST *before* incorporating. Requires a sabbatical or quitting.
3. After incorporating: Start?Zuschuss!, and a BayDiGuP pilot if a call opens.
4. Pre-seed equity.

Applying for FLÜGGE requires a state-Bavarian Hochschule affiliation. Nova SBE does not count; a co-founder could provide it.
