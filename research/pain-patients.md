## Findings
- **The family carries home care, and the support system around it is under-used and hard to navigate. This is the strongest patient-side problem.** In 2025, 9 in 10 people needing care lived at home, and 59.6% of applicants chose Pflegegeld (cash, care organised by family) [A] ([MD Bund, 15 Jul 2026](https://md-bund.de/fileadmin/dokumente/Pressemitteilungen/2026/2026_07_15/_26-07-15_FIN_PM_Report_Pflegebeduerftigkeit.pdf)). Only "3 Prozent der Anspruchsberechtigten" use the statutory care counselling, and about a third of non-users say they do not know it exists [A] ([GKV-SV §7a report, Jun 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/pflegeversicherung/beratung_und_betreuung/pflegeberatung/2026-06_Bericht_des_GKV-Spitzenverbandes_7a_Absatz_9_SGB_XI.pdf); [GKV-SV PM, 21 Jul 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/gkv_spitzenverband/presse/pressemitteilungen_und_statements/pressemitteilung_2292352.jsp)). In 2022, only 38.4% of Pflegegeld recipients used the €125/month (now €131) relief budget [B] ([AOK G+G citing BMG/WIdO](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/magazine/gesundheit-gesellschaft-07-2024/pflegebetrag/)). **Counter-evidence:** WIdO's 2025 survey finds that the main stated reasons for non-use are "fehlender Bedarf" and not wanting care from strangers, not cost [B] ([WIdO, 11 May 2026](https://www.wido.de/fileadmin/user_upload/20260511_WIdO-PM_WIdOmonitor_Pflegende_Angehoerige.pdf)).
- **Carers are overloaded and lose working hours.** 59% of family carers are employed. 45.2% of part-timers cut their hours because of caring, 46.9% of employed carers are "hoch belastet", more than 1 in 10 care ≥40 h/week, and only 12.5% have used the statutory care-leave options [B] ([WIdO 2026](https://www.wido.de/fileadmin/user_upload/20260511_WIdO-PM_WIdOmonitor_Pflegende_Angehoerige.pdf)). In the EU, "80% of all care is provided by informal carers" [B] ([Eurocarers](https://eurocarers.org/about-carers/)).
- **Families cannot find care capacity.** 91% of 150 home-care services had turned away new clients in the previous 6 months, "teils jede zweite Anfrage" [B] ([DEVAP survey, Jan 2023](https://mitglieder.diakonie-hessen.de/fileadmin/user_upload/2023_03_06__Auswertung_DEVAP-Umfrage_zur_Versorgungssicherheit_im_Januar_2023.pdf); provider association, small sample). Care-home operators: "Wer acht Heime abtelefonieren muss, um überhaupt auf eine Warteliste zu kommen …" [B] ([Ärzteblatt, Sep 2024](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/nachrichten/154250/Pflegearbeitgeber-warnen-vor-Mangel-an-Heimplaetzen)). Households that use these services pay out of pocket: on average €469/month, €677 for short-term care. WIdO: "bezogen auf diejenigen, die diese in Anspruch nehmen" and "Die Angaben beruhen auf einer Selbsteinschätzung" (**among users only, self-estimate**; not an average across carer households) [B] ([WIdO 2026](https://www.wido.de/fileadmin/user_upload/20260511_WIdO-PM_WIdOmonitor_Pflegende_Angehoerige.pdf)).
- **Specialist access is a mass-market pain with digital workarounds.** "25 Prozent der Patienten" (respondents with a specialist visit, not all insured) wait >30 days for a specialist, and 30% found getting an appointment "schwierig/sehr schwierig" (35% among those in poor health). 65% still book specialists by phone or in person, while 50% now rate online booking as important (31% in 2022) [B] ([GKV-SV, 5 Jan 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/gkv_spitzenverband/presse/pressemitteilungen_und_statements/pressemitteilung_2181888.jsp); release dated "BERLIN, 05.01.2026", fieldwork 21 Feb–25 Mar 2025, online panel, n=3,520). The government puts the 2024 average wait at 42 days, 9 days longer than in 2019 [B] ([AOK G+G, 7 Apr 2026](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/update/vergabe-von-facharztterminen/)). Doctolib's iOS app has 205,170 ratings (avg 4.79) [C] ([App Store API](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=doctolib&country=de&entity=software&limit=6)).
- **Standalone ePA/ident apps draw heavy onboarding complaints; the size of the problem is unknown.** Only ~8% of insured actively use the ePA [B] ([zm-online/BfDI, 2026](https://www.zm-online.de/news/detail/rund-acht-prozent-der-gkv-versicherten-nutzt-die-epa-aktiv)). Standalone insurer ePA/ident apps are rated 1.3–2.5 stars. In the 300 most recent reviews of three of them, 132 low-star reviews mention identification, login or registration [C] (App Store, tally below). **Selection caveat:** these are the worst-rated apps, and TK-Ident exists only for identification, so login complaints are close to built in. The same insurers' main service apps score 4.5–4.8 (e.g. Meine BARMER 4.64, DAK App 4.64, TK-App 4.76) [C] (App Store search API, 2026-11-07; table in P10).
- **Medication shortages hit chronic patients repeatedly.** In 2025, 1,514 shortage notifications covered 1,041 shortages. Antipsychotics stayed short for 230 days on average, affecting a drug group taken by about 1m patients [B] ([Zi, Jan 2026](https://www.zi.de/fileadmin/Downloads/Service/Medien/Grafik_des_Monats/2026/gdm-lieferengpaesse-01-26.pdf)). 29% of Germans have experienced a pharmacy that could neither dispense nor order a drug [B] ([Ärzteblatt/Forsa, Dec 2024](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/nachrichten/156199/Forsa-Umfrage-Lieferengpaesse-bei-Arzneimitteln-haeufige-Alltagserfahrung)). *Inference:* the budget owner is the pharmacy, wholesaler or manufacturer, not the patient, so this is A5 territory.
- **Caution for "navigator" ideas:** a scoping review of 31 Innovation Fund navigator projects found that "In most cases, no effect was observed" across 153 endpoints. Most of the 153 endpoints showed no effect (the review counts endpoints, not projects). Positive effects showed mostly in patient-reported measures, and 5 projects showed higher costs [A, abstract read via Europe PMC] ([Bundesgesundheitsblatt 2026, PMID 41419645](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:41419645%20AND%20SRC:MED&format=json&resultType=core)). Patients like human navigation, but payers have no proof that it saves money.

## Details

### P1 Navigating home-care benefits
- **Scale [A]:** "über 3 Millionen Pflegebegutachtungen" in 2025. 59.6% of applicants applied for Pflegegeld, 11.2% for professional home care, 19.3% for a combination and 9.4% for a care home. 21.3% of first assessments found no care grade yet ([MD Bund, 15 Jul 2026](https://md-bund.de/fileadmin/dokumente/Pressemitteilungen/2026/2026_07_15/_26-07-15_FIN_PM_Report_Pflegebeduerftigkeit.pdf)).
- **Counselling barely used [A]:** "Die Inanspruchnahme der Pflegeberatung liegt bei 3 Prozent der Anspruchsberechtigten" ([GKV-SV §7a report, Jun 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/pflegeversicherung/beratung_und_betreuung/pflegeberatung/2026-06_Bericht_des_GKV-Spitzenverbandes_7a_Absatz_9_SGB_XI.pdf)). About "einem knappen Drittel" of non-users don't know about it. Video counselling is offered by >25% of counselling centres but used in "unter 1 Prozent" of cases. In 2% of home visits, care was found "nicht sichergestellt", often because carers were overloaded ([GKV-SV PM, 21 Jul 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/gkv_spitzenverband/presse/pressemitteilungen_und_statements/pressemitteilung_2292352.jsp)).
- **Relief budget (Entlastungsbetrag) [B]:** BMG reports €2.95bn spent in 2023, i.e. "rund 55 Prozent" of home-care recipients used it. WIdO (2022): 76.7% of recipients of professional services but only 38.4% of Pflegegeld recipients used it. VdK and the consumer association vzbv call it "zu bürokratisch, zu kompliziert und zu gering"; providers are scarce, families must pay upfront and wait for reimbursement ([AOK G+G](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/magazine/gesundheit-gesellschaft-07-2024/pflegebetrag/)). Current amount: "bis zu 131 Euro pro Monat"; short-term + respite care share "bis zu 3.539,00 Euro" per year [A] ([BMG Leistungsbeträge 2025](https://bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/P/Pflegeversicherung_Leistungsbeitraege/Uebersicht_Leistungsbetraege_2025.pdf)).
- **VdK study [B] (advocacy; Hochschule Osnabrück, commissioned 2020; >14,000 respondents in Bavaria):** in Bavaria 84% don't use the relief budget ("vier Prozent mehr als bundesweit"), 93% found no day care, 87% never had short-term care. "Jährlich verfallen … Leistungsansprüche … im Wert von mindestens zwölf Milliarden Euro". With counselling, use of the relief budget rose "von 18 auf 82 Prozent" ([VdK Bayern](https://bayern.vdk.de/assets/lv-bayern/Dokumente/Unsere_Angebote/Leben_im_Alter/VdK_Pflegestudie/Artikel_VdK-Pflegestudie_Regionalauswertung_Bayern.pdf)). The €12bn is a VdK estimate. It conflicts with BMG's 55% use rate, so it should not be used for sizing.
- **Counter-evidence [B]:** among Pflegegeld-only households, 55.2% use no other support, but the main reasons are "fehlender Bedarf" (43.2% for home-care services, 52% for short-term care) and not wanting strangers (46.5% for home-care services). "Finanzielle Ursachen spielen … nur eine untergeordnete Rolle" ([WIdO 2026](https://www.wido.de/fileadmin/user_upload/20260511_WIdO-PM_WIdOmonitor_Pflegende_Angehoerige.pdf)). *Inference:* the "information gap" story (VdK) and the "preference" story (WIdO) both have support. A venture must test which one dominates.
- **Assessment disputes [B]:** 29.6% of objections against first assessments succeeded in each of 2020–2022 (28.3% in Q1–3 2023). First assessments rose from 700,000 (2011) to ~1.15m (2022) ([Ärzteblatt, Dec 2023](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/nachrichten/148119/Widerspruch-gegen-die-Pflegegradeinstufung-oft-erfolgreich); [sonntagsblatt/epd](https://www.sonntagsblatt.de/artikel/epd/fast-jeder-dritte-widerspruch-gegen-pflegegrad-erfolgreich)). Data ~3 years old.
- **Online voices [C]:** in the rheuma-online subforum "Krankenkassen und Pflegeversicherung", 8 of 21 threads on page 1 concern care-grade applications, MD assessments or the relief budget ([index](https://forum.rheuma-online.de/forums/krankenkassen-und-pflegeversicherung.51/)). Excerpts: "verstehe ich das mit dem Entlastungsbetrag nicht ganz" ([thread](https://forum.rheuma-online.de/threads/frage-zum-entlastungsbetrag.70019/)). On a phone assessment: "Das wirft bei mir natürlich viele Fragen auf" ([thread](https://forum.rheuma-online.de/threads/mdk-telefon-begutachtung-erfahrungen.69869/)). Counter-signal: a 2025 thread reports an upgrade decided within days, "sehr schnell und unkompliziert" ([thread](https://forum.rheuma-online.de/threads/pflegegrad-1-verschlechterung-beantragt.72569/)). Several threads date from 2020–21 (Corona era).
- **Who pays (inference):** Pflegekassen already fund counselling (§7a) and the relief budget, so a service that raises correct use spends payer money. Kassen may resist this in a deficit year (SPV gap €7.6bn in 2027 per A1). Possible buyers are commercial care providers billing the relief budget, employers, or the Kassen' own counselling mandate.

### P2 Finding care capacity
- **Home-care services [B]:** DEVAP (Jan 2023, 501 facilities, 150 home-care services): "91% mussten in den letzten 6 Monaten Neukunden ablehnen, teils jede zweite Anfrage", and 73% refused to increase services for existing clients. Filling skilled home-care posts takes "9-12 Monate" ([DEVAP](https://mitglieder.diakonie-hessen.de/fileadmin/user_upload/2023_03_06__Auswertung_DEVAP-Umfrage_zur_Versorgungssicherheit_im_Januar_2023.pdf)). An NRW sample (2018, stale) counted 2,600 refusals in one month, extrapolated to ~10,000 ([Ärzteblatt 2018](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/news/pflegedienste-in-nordrhein-westfalen-muessen-senioren-abweisen-1c7f9b17-fbec-4e1a-877d-619625619744)).
- **Short-term and day care [B]:** in the VdK Bavaria data, insufficient capacity is cited for short-term care by 64% and for day/night care by 50%. About 30% are put off by the application process ([VdK Bayern](https://bayern.vdk.de/assets/lv-bayern/Dokumente/Unsere_Angebote/Leben_im_Alter/VdK_Pflegestudie/Artikel_VdK-Pflegestudie_Regionalauswertung_Bayern.pdf)).
- **Care homes [B]:** AGVP says "Bis 2040 benötigen wir jedes Jahr rund 17.000 zusätzliche Pflegeheimplätze" ([Ärzteblatt, Sep 2024](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/nachrichten/154250/Pflegearbeitgeber-warnen-vor-Mangel-an-Heimplaetzen)). In Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, >1,200 of 25,000 places cannot be filled for lack of staff ([Ärzteblatt, Mar 2024](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/news/hunderte-plaetze-in-pflegeheimen-aus-personalmangel-nicht-nutzbar-754aaaaa-5db9-4e45-b7b5-d4a3d0ac3c9e)).
- **Counterpoint `[UNVERIFIED]`, snippet only:** a vdek Lower Saxony paper argues that refusals by several providers do not equal undersupply.
- **Online voices:** forum threads were not reachable (alzheimer-forum 403, pflege-forum 502). Search snippets showed provider reviews of the type "keine Kapazitäten mehr" (kennstdueinen.de, snippet only, not counted).
- *Inference:* the root cause is staff. Software can match demand to free capacity (e.g. vacancy exchanges like the "Marktplatz ambulante Pflege Münster", seen in a snippet only), but it cannot create capacity.

### P3 Carer overload and work–care conflict
- [B] WIdO forsa survey (n≈1,500, Sep–Oct 2025): 59% employed (31.3% full-time, 27.7% part-time). 45.2% of part-timers reduced hours due to caring. 46.9% of employed and 38.3% of non-employed carers are highly burdened, and >40% overall. 63.4% care daily; >1 in 10 care ≥40 h/week; ~40% have cared for ≥5 years; 60.4% are women. Only 12.5% used Pflegezeit/Familienpflegezeit, and ~1 in 4 non-users cite money ([WIdO 2026](https://www.wido.de/fileadmin/user_upload/20260511_WIdO-PM_WIdOmonitor_Pflegende_Angehoerige.pdf)).
- [A] 3.1m people are cared for mainly by relatives (Destatis, via `research/trends.md`).
- [B] ~1.84m people live with dementia; 364,000–445,000 new cases in 2023; 2.3–2.7m people aged 65+ by 2050 ([DAlzG, 4 Sep 2024](https://deutsche-alzheimer.de/fileadmin/Alz/pdf/pressemitteilungen/2024_Pressemitteilungen/2024-09-04_pm_dalzg_informationsblatt_haeufigkeiten.pdf)). The share cared for at home is `[UNVERIFIED]` (snippets give both two-thirds and three-quarters).
- [B] EU: "80% of all care is provided by informal carers"; its value is estimated at ~2.5% of EU GDP vs 1.7% public LTC spending in 2019 ([Eurocarers](https://eurocarers.org/about-carers/)). Advocacy source, primary study not opened.
- [C] krebs-kompass "Forum für Angehörige": 104 threads on the first two index pages; 18 titles explicitly ask for orientation or help (e.g. "wie kann ich ihr helfen", "Was können wir tun?", "Brauche Hilfe!!!") ([index](https://www.krebs-kompass.de/forumdisplay.php?f=49)).
- **Who pays (inference):** no clear statutory budget for carer support beyond SPV benefits. Employers carry the cost of reduced hours, which makes them a possible B2B2C buyer (`[UNVERIFIED]`: no DE employer-spend data found).

### P4 Specialist appointment access
- [B] GKV-SV representative survey (n=3,520, Feb–Mar 2025): "25 Prozent der Patienten warten länger als 30 Tage", half get an appointment within 10 days; 30% found it difficult (35% in poor health); 50% rate online/app booking as important (2022: 31%); 65% book specialists in person or by phone ([GKV-SV](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/gkv_spitzenverband/presse/pressemitteilungen_und_statements/pressemitteilung_2181888.jsp)).
- [B] Government figure: 42 days average wait in 2024, +9 days vs 2019. The Federal Audit Office (BRH) puts the TSVG bonus fees at €2.9bn extra cost by mid-2024 "ohne erkennbaren Nutzen". The KBV warns of "50 Tage und mehr" under the reform proposals. A primary-care (gatekeeping) bill is planned ([AOK G+G, Apr 2026](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/update/vergabe-von-facharztterminen/)).
- [B] Zi: appointment-service (TSS) cases 1,472,578 and GP-referral cases 4,491,089, i.e. 0.4% and 1.3% of 345m specialist cases ([Zi insights, May 2026](https://www.zi.de/fileadmin/Downloads/Service/Veranstaltungen/Zi_insights/Zi_insights_Terminvermittlung_2026-05-27_Mangiapane.pdf)).
- [C] 116117.app (2,052 ratings, avg 4.14): of its 100 most recent reviews (Sep 2023–Nov 2026), 61 are ≤2 stars, and 26 of those mention "Termin". Examples: "Ich verstehe nicht welche Ärzte findet man hier überhaupt…", "Buchung in der App nicht möglich" ([iTunes RSS](https://itunes.apple.com/de/rss/customerreviews/id=1465237675/sortBy=mostRecent/json)). Doctolib 205,170 ratings (4.79) and Jameda 10,762 (4.68) show heavy adoption of the private workaround ([App Store API](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=116117&country=de&entity=software&limit=6)). rheuma-online thread "Empfehlenswerte Rheumatologen gesucht" runs to 23+ pages ([index](https://forum.rheuma-online.de/forums/arzt-und-klinik-reha-suche.65/)).
- *Inference:* the pain is real, but booking is crowded (Doctolib, Jameda, 116117) and the binding constraint is capacity and the budget cap ("40 Millionen Facharzttermine nicht vergütet", KBV, same G+G source). Triage/steering under the planned primary-care law is the policy opening (see A1/A9).

### P5 Psychotherapy waiting times
- [B] vdek claims analysis (1.5m records, 2022–2024): after the mandatory first consultation, 80% of adults and 85% of children wait <4 weeks for a session; 61% of adults and 55% of minors wait >2 weeks (target 14 days); half of minors with urgent need wait >20 days ([AOK G+G, 17 Jun 2026](https://www.aok.de/pp/gg/update/psychotherapie-wartezeiten/)).
- [B] BPtK compilation (2022): BPtK 2018 survey, 19.9 weeks from first enquiry to treatment; BPtK analysis of 2019 data, ~40% wait 3–9 months to start treatment after the consultation ([BPtK PM, 7 Jun 2022](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/20220607_pm_bptk_Viel_zu_lange_Wartezeiten_in_der_ambulanten_Psychotherapie_55a8908172.pdf)). **Data >3 years old.** BPtK (2025) opposes a mandatory GP gate, saying it "erhöht Wartezeiten" ([BPtK, 22 Aug 2025](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/20250822_pm_bptk_Positionspapier_Direktzugang_Psychotherapie_e96847b13e.pdf)).
- **Contested:** payer data (weeks) and the profession's surveys (months) measure different intervals.
- [C] `[UNVERIFIED]`: search snippets only (e.g. a student contacting 90 therapists). No forum thread opened.
