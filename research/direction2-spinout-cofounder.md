# Direction 2 desk run: business co-founder of a Munich research spin-out
Task: C20 | Date: 2026-11-18 | Status: draft

Question: can the CEO join a Munich health research spin-out as the business co-founder, with a grant paying the salary from about September 2027 (direction 2 in `research/new-directions.md`)? Which projects, which grants, what timing, what profile? Grades per `CLAUDE.md` rule 2. *My math* and *inference* are labelled. No one was contacted.

## Findings
- **The timing fits September 2027 exactly, and the grant pays a salary.** The federal spin-out grant EXIST-Forschungstransfer (EFT) reviews applications in batches. Applications in by **31 March 2027** go to the jury in June, and the handbook's "optimal start" is **1 September 2027**. The next batch (31 Aug 2027) starts 1 March 2028 [A] ([EFT handbook 2026](https://exist.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/260716_Handbuch_EFT_2026_barrierefrei.pdf)). The team is employed by the university, "nach TV-L, TVöD oder Haustarif" (same handbook). A TV-L E13 post pays **€4,759.37–5,106.09 a month gross** full-time (levels 1–2, valid 1 Apr 2026–28 Feb 2027) [B] ([TV-L table, UK Würzburg staff council](https://www.ukw.de/fileadmin/uk/personalrat/Tarifinformationen/2026/Entgelttabelle_Arbeitnehmer_2026.pdf)). New applications are accepted only "bis zum 31. Dezember 2027" under the current rules [A] ([EFT guideline 2024](https://exist.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Richtlinie-EFT-03-07-2024.pdf)).
- **The business seat is required by law, and funded teams mostly found companies.** Every EFT team needs "mindestens eine Person mit betriebswirtschaftlicher beziehungsweise unternehmerischer Kompetenz", and all members need at least a Master's degree (one person may have a Bachelor's) [A] (guideline). Of funded projects, **"knapp 87%"** founded a company, and **">96%"** of those still exist five years after funding ends [A] ([EFT monitoring report 2025](https://exist.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/EFT_Monitoringbericht_2025_Nr.3.pdf)). Munich is a top host: **TUM 127 applications / 42 approved** and **LMU 40 / 17**, cumulative since the programme began (same report) [A]. *My math:* 33 % and 43 %. Like the national 27.8 %, these totals include pre-2023 outlines, so they understate today's odds (critic note, 2026-11-18).
- **Public "business co-founder wanted" ads for Munich health research projects are almost absent: 0 confirmed.** I found 6 co-founder ads in total: 1 opened and health-related but in Berlin (KIARA, Fraunhofer HHI, on the LMU board [B]), 2 Munich health ads seen only as search snippets `[UNVERIFIED]`, and 3 outside health. *Inference:* in Munich, research teams find business people through university transfer offices, programmes and personal networks, not job boards. The CEO cannot apply to a list. He has to be visible in 2–3 of these channels, and that is an outward action.
- **Three Munich-relevant programmes need a business person in 2027 and can pay one.** (1) **Helmholtz Enterprise** (2026 2nd call; jury 13 Nov 2026; projects start from 1 Feb 2027) "also finances externally found team members". From month 9 (about Oct 2027) it requires "a complete team … with CEO competence" [A] ([Helmholtz call, 13 Jul 2026](https://www.helmholtz.de/assets/helmholtz_gemeinschaft/user_upload/Ausschreibungen/2026/01_Helmholtz_Enterprise_2026_2nd_Call.pdf)). (2) **m4 Award 2027** (Bavaria, up to €500,000 per project): short descriptions due **30 Nov 2026**, project start **Nov 2027**; the application has a "Founder team" section [B] ([BioM info event, 13 Oct 2026](https://www.m4-award.org/fileadmin/Webdata/Uploads/Gruendung/m4_Award/m4_Award_2027/Praesentation_-_m4_Award_2027_-_Informationsveranstaltung_-_Stufe_1_-_20261013.pdf)). (3) **EFT** itself (above).
- **Part-time while employed: allowed only in narrow ways.** EFT allows split posts ("Eine Vollzeitstelle kann auch z.B. von 2 Personen mit einer jeweiligen Halbtagsstelle wahrgenommen werden"). But the guideline justifies part-time with "Vereinbarkeit von Beruf und Familie in begründeten Fällen", and all members "müssen bei der antragstellenden Hochschule angestellt sein" [A] (handbook, guideline). Whether a half-time EFT post may sit next to the CEO's full-time Lisbon job is not addressed `[UNVERIFIED]`. *Inference:* the realistic model is to help write the application before September 2027 without pay, then start full-time on 1 Sep 2027. One part-time-compatible entry route exists. UnternehmerTUM's "Future Entrepreneur" track matches business people with incubator start-ups at **15–20 hours a week**, mostly online in the late afternoon; the next application phase "starts in December" [B] ([UnternehmerTUM](https://incubator.unternehmertum.de/future-entrepreneurs/)). It is not health-specific.
- **What a business co-founder must bring: the CEO meets the formal bar but not the experienced profile that explicit ads ask for.** Formal (EFT): Master's degree ✔; "Managementqualifikation" ✔/?; the handbook checklist asks for a business degree ✔, "Vorherige Gründungen oder Geschäftsführungsrollen" ✘, "Vermarktungserfahrung" ✘, industry knowledge "durch konkrete Beispiele" ~ [A]. The one open health seat (KIARA) asks for "proven experience in medtech or healthtech commercialization, an established healthcare network, and a track record in securing venture capital" [B] ([LMU IEC](https://www.iec.lmu.de/en/our-services/incubation-transfer/startup-jobs/)). Max Planck's business support for spin-offs is "Industry experts" (freelancers or industry staff, "on a fee basis", typically 3–5 days) and "Interim managers" [B] ([Max-Planck-Innovation EMI](https://www.max-planck-innovation.com/spin-off/service-spectrum/expertise-meets-innovation.html)). *Inference:* the CEO's edge is AI implementation. His best targets are AI-heavy projects (diagnostic software, AI drug-design platforms) where he can do more than the business plan.
- **Verdict (inference): direction 2 is real but cannot be advanced by desk research alone.** A full venture brief does not fit, because the problem is the partner team's to choose. What fits is a short "entry pack" plus 2–3 approved low-cost outward steps before **28 Feb 2027**, the last point to be written into a 31 March 2027 application. The 5 best-matching projects are in §4. None is confirmed to be seeking a business co-founder.

### 6. What would make this fail (kill lines, inference)
- **K-1 (date, observable):** by **28 Feb 2027** the CEO is not named in any draft EFT, Helmholtz Enterprise or m4 application. Then the 1 Sep 2027 start is off. The next EFT start is 1 Mar 2028, a six-month gap after the planned job exit.
- **K-2:** the EFT rules after 31 Dec 2027 are unknown. The ESF+ co-funding period is "2021 bis 2027" [A] (handbook ch. 9). A slip into 2028 depends on a new guideline `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- **K-3:** the CEO does not choose the problem. If the only teams on offer are wet-lab therapeutics (most m4/EFT health projects), his AI edge is unused. Then the role is a 2–3-year business-plan and fundraising job, and investors judge him against experienced biotech managers.
