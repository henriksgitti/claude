# Review: research/scoring.md (Stage B framework)
Date: 2026-11-06 | Verdict: needs work (before B1 runs)

This is a method file, not research, so there are no sources to check. These are logic issues that will distort B1 if not fixed. It came from the restructuring commit, so changes are best confirmed by the CEO (see Open question in `progress.md`).

## Critical issues (must fix before B1)
1. **Purely additive scoring lets strengths hide a fatal weakness.** A problem with "Unclear who pays" (criterion 3 = 1) can still reach the top 5 on founder fit, future relevance and pain. In healthcare the payer question is the most common killer. Add **knock-out rules**: e.g. criterion 3 ≤2 or criterion 5 ≤2 → not eligible for the top 5, whatever the total.
2. **Criterion 3 example contradicts current evidence.** "reimbursement path (e.g. DiGA)" is listed as a 5-level example. trends.md (GKV-SV, FinanzKommission) shows DiGA tightening: 16 of 74 delisted, price negotiation from day 1 being pushed. A DiGA path should not score 5 by default. Replace it with "budget owner already spends on this category (evidence: tenders, existing contracts, funded programme)".
3. **No evidence-confidence dimension.** A score of 4 based on one [B] article counts the same as a 4 based on [A] data. Add a confidence tag per score (H/M/L), and break ties or demote on L. Otherwise B1 will reward the best-written problems, not the best-evidenced ones.

## Other issues
- **Criterion 1 depends on "many independent online signals"**, but Reddit is blocked (A2), so patient-side problems with forum evidence will look stronger than B2B problems whose pain shows up in tenders and job ads. Define pain evidence per buyer type (patients: forums/reviews; providers: kununu, job ads, tenders, surveys).
- **Criterion 2 "≥€1bn addressable in EU"** has no method, which invites TAM inflation. Require a bottom-up count (number of buyers × realistic price) with sources. Top-down % of health spending is not allowed.
- **Founder fit (2) and achievability (5)** both reward "small team, no deep science", so they partly double-count. Acceptable, but note it.
- **Missing criterion: sales cycle / adoption capacity.** Hospital procurement (EU tenders) and care services with weak IT (50% SMC-B activation, trends.md) can make "first customer in 12 months" unrealistic even when budget exists. Either fold this into criterion 5 with an explicit check, or add it.
- **Who pays vs who uses:** require each scored problem to name the user, the buyer and the budget line separately. Otherwise criterion 3 gets scored on the user's pain.
