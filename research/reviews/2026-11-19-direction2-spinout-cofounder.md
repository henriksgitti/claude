## Minor issues (for the researcher if the file is revised; not a task)
1. **"TUM's portal is offline, so current TUM ads could not be searched" (Details §3, Gaps) is only half right.** The cited page itself says "Die Stellenangebote aus portal.mytum.de finden Sie vorläufig in englischer Fassung HIER" and links `jobs.mytum.de/jobs/index_html_en.html`; its "Academic staff" list (`/jobs/wissenschaftler.html`) loads with ~235 ads (24 pages), newest dated 29.10.2026. The critic searched the 40 newest (1–29 Oct 2026) for founder / Gründ / EXIST / spin-off / business: **0 business co-founder ads**. So the file's conclusion (no visible Munich health business seat) holds for TUM too, but the list was searchable. The mirror appears frozen at 29 Oct 2026.
2. **SYNTRA row: "plans to transfer the technology into a spin-off" (search summary) is graded [B].** The opened [Helmholtz Munich page](https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/newsroom/news-all/artikel/m4-award-2025-goes-to-helmholtz-munich-for-rna-therapy-innovation) (3 Jul 2025) names the team (group leader, two professors, a PhD candidate, a postdoc; no business person) and the programme's spin-off aim, but no SYNTRA-specific spin-off plan and no SYNTRA amount ("up to €500,000 per project" is the programme cap). The plan should carry `[UNVERIFIED]`; "up to €500,000" is fine as a cap.
3. **Helmholtz Enterprise timing is one review earlier than stated.** Findings say the "complete team … with CEO competence" is required "from month 9 (about Oct 2027)". The call says the jury reviews "after month seven" and the complete team is a "prerequisite for further funding in months 9–14". For a 1 Feb 2027 start the CEO seat must be filled for the month-7 review, i.e. about **Aug–Sep 2027**, not Oct. This still fits a 1 Sep 2027 start, but it tightens kill line K-1.
4. **The salary line assumes the business seat is paid at E13.** The handbook says pay follows "TV-L, TVöD oder Haustarif" and "Tarifvertrag, Eingruppierung und Stufe müssen begründet werden, zum Beispiel mit vorheriger Beschäftigung oder besonderer Qualifikation". E13 is the usual academic grade but is not stated for the business member; Helmholtz centres pay TVöD, not TV-L. The cited table is valid only to 28 Feb 2027 (a raise follows). Label "E13" as an assumption in Findings, as §1 already does for level 2.
5. **Three small source mismatches.** (a) NOVA Startup Competition also requires "a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) already developed" and investment-readiness within 12 months ([regulation](https://novainnovation.unl.pt/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NOVA-Startup-Competition-Regulation-2026.pdf), eligibility e); the file lists only the alumni rule, which overstates the route's fit. (b) imuveo is listed as "three scientists named"; the [TUM VL page](https://www.tum-venture-labs.de/teams/imuveo/) says the founding team combines "translational immunology, radiopharmaceutical development, and entrepreneurship", so a business competence is claimed in-team. (c) The TUM VL Venture Manager job page does not mention "~100 teams"; that number is on the Healthcare lab page.
6. **Grade: EIT Jumpstarter T&C tagged [A].** A programme's terms are an official document of the organiser but not a regulator or statistic; [B] fits CLAUDE.md rule 2 better. Not built on.
7. **Trivia (no action):** Meetup now shows 904 members (file 903) and the 1 Dec 2026 event at **16:45** CET (file "17:00–18:00"). FormulAIte's date 20 Jul 2026 is on the opened LMU page itself, not only in a search summary.
