## Critical issues (must fix)
None that change the conclusion. Fixes:
1. **"SCE Incubation … takes applications on a rolling basis" is not supported.** The SCE page shows a fixed "Deadline: 2. August 2026" (already passed) and says nothing about rolling intake. The page does say "mindestens 10 Stunden pro Woche", which supports the part-time claim. The recommendation "Now, part-time: … SCE or UnternehmerTUM Incubator" therefore needs the next intake date. UnternehmerTUM incubator: cohorts start "2 Mal pro Jahr (März und September)", 10-12 weeks, free, target group includes "Berufstätige" (supported); "takes no equity" is not on the page (not supported).
2. **"Capital is available … favour B2B workflow AI" is thin and omits the downside.** EY: Bayern "mehr als 1,1 Mrd. Euro (minus 46 Prozent)" - down 46 % year on year - and 93 deals (+21 %) (supported). The Findings leave out the -46 %. Health is rank 4-5 nationally ("jenseits der 450-Millionen-Euro-Marke", supported). "Munich health deals in 2026 favour B2B workflow AI" rests on two deals (Calliora, Avelios); no deal count or list. Treat as anecdote, not trend.
3. **FLÜGGE "funded up to six projects" is a plan, not an outcome.** The 2025/1 call says "Es ist beabsichtigt, bis zu sechs Projekte … zu fördern", ~€600,000 planned; deadline 10.03.2025. The table's "Current call open through December 18, 2026" cites a Bayern Innovativ page I did not open; the PDF cited is the 2025 call. Fix wording and cite the current call.
