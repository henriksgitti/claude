For use at events and in transfer-office calls. One block per project, at most one page each. Grades: [A] official / regulator / funder, [B] university, company, news, industry report, [C] forum. *Inference* = my reading, not a sourced fact. Agents contacted nobody.

## Findings
- **None of the 5 projects has a founded company or a public business co-founder decision as of 20 Nov 2026.** ki-alz-2 and RadioGene-2 ended their GO-Bio feasibility grants on 30 Sep 2026 [A]; GO-Bio says such projects should continue in "VIP+, EXIST research transfer, KMU-innovativ or GO-Bio next" ([GO-Bio initial](https://www.go-bio.de/gobio/en/go-bio/go-bio-initial/go-bio-initial_node.html) [A]). GO-Bio next takes outlines on **15 March** and 15 September ([GO-Bio next](https://www.go-bio.de/gobio/en/go-bio/go-bio-next/go-bio-next_node.html) [A]). So 15 Mar 2027 (GO-Bio next) and 31 Mar 2027 (EFT) are the next dates when either team would need a business person. *Inference.*
- **Best fit for your AI background: ki-alz-2 (AI diagnosis software, Munich) and KIARA (AI in the operating room, Berlin, the only seat publicly open).** The other three are lab biotech (RNA delivery, PET tracers). In those, the business job is licensing deals with pharma companies and raising money, judged against experienced biotech managers. *Inference.*
- **ki-alz-2's own final report puts drug companies, CROs and research sites first, not hospital routine care,** and classifies the software as a class IIa medical device ([concept-phase report](https://oa.tib.eu/renate/bitstreams/d56546b7-826a-486d-8e01-97e3170c2640/download) [B]). The demand driver is weak in Germany: the G-BA found "no added benefit" for both new Alzheimer antibodies (lecanemab Feb 2026, donanemab Apr 2026), price talks were still running on 4 Nov 2026, and a neurologist estimates only 1,000–2,000 people treated so far ([PZ, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.pharmazeutische-zeitung.de/erfahrungen-nach-einem-jahr-in-der-anwendung-171690/) [B]).
- **The biotech projects sell to drug developers through licence deals worth up to $100–300m on paper, paid mostly as milestones.** Example: the delivery company Genevant can earn "up to $107 million in upfront and contingent milestone payments per product" ([Repair Bio, Sep 2024](https://www.repairbiotechnologies.com/repair-biotechnologies-and-genevant-sciences-to-collaborate-to-develop-mrna-lnp-therapy-with-first-in-class-potential-to-turn-back-atherosclerosis/) [B]). There are 1,370 RNA therapies and 2,217 gene therapies in development worldwide ([Citeline Q2 2026](https://images.info.citeline.com/Web/Citeline/%7Bc8541915-5eb6-4053-b6eb-e5a3ead9bb98%7D_report_clinical_asgct-citeline-q2-2026.pdf) [B]). Well-funded US competitors exist: Aera $193m, Nvelop $100m [B].
- **KIARA competes with well-funded firms:** Caresyntax raised $180m in Aug 2024 ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2024/08/15/caresyntax-raises-180m-surgical-data-management-platform) [B]). KIARA's own ad asks for medtech sales, a hospital network and VC fundraising experience ([ad PDF](https://cms-cdn.lmu.de/media/04-iec/iec/downloads/kiara-cofounder-wanted-long-pdf.pdf) [B]). The ad was still listed on 20 Nov 2026; the PDF was created on 25 Jun 2026 (file metadata), so the seat has probably been open about 5 months (*inference*).

## Details

### 1. ki-alz-2 — AI for early Alzheimer's diagnosis (LMU Klinikum, Neuroradiology)
**What it does.** Software that combines brain scans, memory tests and other data into one AI result: is it early Alzheimer's, and which kind of dementia? It explains why it reached its answer ("Explainable AI"). Concept phase 2023–24 (€119,993.29), feasibility phase 1 Oct 2024–30 Sep 2026 (€599,632.80). The feasibility phase was meant to confirm "under realistic hospital conditions" what had worked "under controlled study conditions", as "the basis for a spinoff" ([GO-Bio](https://go-bio.de/gobio/en/projects/go-bio-initial_eng/_documents/ki-alz_eng.html) [A]). Project lead: Dr. Boris-Stephan Rauchmann; team member Prof. Robert Perneczky ([report](https://oa.tib.eu/renate/bitstreams/d56546b7-826a-486d-8e01-97e3170c2640/download) [B]).

**Who would buy, and what is paid today.**
- The team's own priority: "Pharma, CROs, Forschung"; route "kurzfristig über Pilotpartnerschaften mit klinischen Forschungseinrichtungen und Industriepartnern", routine care only "langfristig"; business model "SaaS-/Lizenzierungsoptionen" (report, file dated May 2025 [B]). No price per analysis is published `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- Germany: about **1.8 million** people lived with dementia at the end of 2025; about **357,000** people aged 65+ developed dementia in 2023 ([DAlzG factsheet](https://www.deutsche-alzheimer.de/fileadmin/alz/pdf/factsheets/infoblatt1_haeufigkeit_demenzerkrankungen_dalzg.pdf), DZNE-based estimates [B]).
- Hospitals and doctors: from 1 Apr 2026 doctors bill lecanemab care with existing EBM codes (infusion, MRI) plus a new ApoE gene test code, GOP 11602 at **€53.76** ([KBV](https://www.kbv.de/praxis/tools-und-services/praxisnachrichten/2026/03-05/verguetung-fuer-neues-alzheimer-medikament-leqembi-geregelt) [A]). I found no billing code for AI analysis of the scans, so a hospital would pay from its own budget `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- Demand risk: both antibodies got "no added benefit" from the G-BA. The decision sets the price talks; the manufacturer could leave the German market if no price is agreed ([Alzheimer Europe](https://www.alzheimer-europe.org/news/gemeinsame-bundesausschuss-finds-no-added-benefit-lecanemab-germany) [B]; [PZ](https://www.pharmazeutische-zeitung.de/erfahrungen-nach-einem-jahr-in-der-anwendung-171690/) [B]).

**Competitors.** mediaire (Berlin; mdbrain MRI AI) raised **€12m** in Nov 2024 and is used in "over 350 hospitals and clinics across Europe" ([HTGF](https://www.htgf.de/?p=117946) [B]). icometrix (Leuven; MRI AI incl. a side-effect module for the antibodies) agreed to be bought by GE HealthCare in Sep 2025 for an undisclosed sum ([MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/ge-healthcare-icometrix-acquire-brain-imaging-software/759861/) [B]). neotiv (Magdeburg; digital memory tests, CE class I) raised €10m in Nov 2022 ([Capricorn](https://capricorn.be/en/news_items/capricorn-partners-joins-in-a-eur-10-million-pre-series-a-financing-round-in-neotiv-to-accelerate-alzheimers-diagnosis) [B], >3 years old). Blood tests may replace some imaging: Roche's Elecsys pTau217 got the CE mark in May 2026 for "both primary and secondary care" ([Euronews](https://euronews.com/health/2026/05/12/roche-gets-european-approval-for-alzheimers-diagnosis-blood-test) [B]).

**Regulatory path and time to revenue.** Software "intended to provide information which is used to take decisions with diagnosis or therapeutic purposes is classified as class IIa" or higher ([MDCG 2019-11 rev.1](https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/b45335c5-1679-4c71-a91c-fc7a4d37f12b_en?filename=mdcg_2019_11_en.pdf), Rule 11 [A]); the team's regulatory workshop reached the same class (report [B]). *Inference:* a class IIa CE mark needs a notified body and clinical evidence, so 2–3 years. Paid research or pharma-trial pilots could start sooner if the use is research only.

**What you would do in the first 12 months (*inference*).** Write the EFT or GO-Bio next business plan; run 10–15 discovery calls with pharma trial teams and CROs to find who pays per trial and how much; set up the regulatory plan and quality system with an outside consultant; build the cloud product with the developers.

**Three questions to ask.**
1. "The feasibility phase ended on 30 Sep: did the hospital validation confirm the study results, and what is the next grant (EFT, GO-Bio next, VIP+)?"
2. "Your report puts pharma and CROs first. Is there a pilot partner or letter of intent yet, and what would they pay per trial?"
3. "With the G-BA's 'no added benefit' and open price talks, how does the case change if the antibodies leave Germany? Is the plan Germany-first or EU/US-first?"

**Status.** No company, news or new grant found after 30 Sep 2026 (web search EN + DE, GO-Bio and LMU pages). Route: LMU Spin-off Service (entry pack).

### 2. SYNTRA / EDGE — AI-designed RNA transporters (Helmholtz Munich + TUM)
**What it does.** A new kind of "delivery vehicle" for RNA and gene-editing medicines, built from natural protein parts plus a protein designed with generative AI. In cell culture the lead candidate (STV-C8) "transports RNA far more efficiently than the lipid nanoparticles tested". In mice it reached mainly the lungs with "no evidence of immunological or toxic side effects". In a pig it cut out "a disease-relevant section of the dystrophin gene". Published in *Nature* on 2 Sep 2026; "The team also plans to transfer the technology into a spin-off company" ([Helmholtz Munich](https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/newsroom/news-all/artikel/ai-designed-proteins-enable-a-new-generation-of-rna-transporters) [B]). First disease: Duchenne muscular dystrophy ([GO-Bio](https://www.go-bio.de/gobio/en/projects/go-bio-eng/_documents/EDGE_Gbn1_en.html) [A]). People: Dr. Christoph Gruber (GO-Bio project lead), Dr. Florian Giesert (group leader), Prof. Wolfgang Wurst.

**Who would buy, and what is paid today.** Drug developers license delivery technology. Precedent: Genevant (lipid nanoparticles) can earn up to $303m from Takeda for up to two rare liver diseases (Mar 2022, [Pharmaceutical Technology](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/news/genevant-takeda-gene-therapies/) [B], >3 years old) and "up to $107 million in upfront and contingent milestone payments per product" plus "mid to high single digits" royalties from Repair Biotechnologies (Sep 2024, [Repair](https://www.repairbiotechnologies.com/repair-biotechnologies-and-genevant-sciences-to-collaborate-to-develop-mrna-lnp-therapy-with-first-in-class-potential-to-turn-back-atherosclerosis/) [B]). Most of these sums are milestones, paid only if the partner's drug progresses. Pipeline: 2,217 gene therapies and 1,370 RNA therapies in development (Citeline Q2 2026 [B]). Duchenne need: the leading gene therapy (Elevidys) got a negative CHMP opinion in the EU in July 2025 ([Roche](https://www.roche.com/media/releases/med-cor-2025-07-25) [B]).

**Competitors.** Aera Therapeutics (protein nanoparticles from human proteins): $193m Series A+B, Feb 2023 ([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/aera-therapeutics-launches-with-193-million-in-financing-to-enable-and-advance-the-next-generation-of-transformative-genetic-medicines) [B], >3 years old). Nvelop Therapeutics (virus-like particles): $100m, Apr 2024 ([GEN](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/genome-editing/cloaking-device-liu-joung-launch-100m-nvelop-therapeutics-to-advance-delivery-of-genetic-cargo/) [B]). Lipid-nanoparticle licensors such as Genevant.

**Regulatory path and time to revenue.** A gene therapy medicine is an advanced therapy medicinal product (ATMP), assessed by EMA's Committee for Advanced Therapies ([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/advanced-therapy-medicinal-products-overview) [A]). GO-Bio next phase I (€3,054,897, 1 Nov 2025–31 Oct 2028) funds "efficacy and safety analyses … to prepare for clinical testing"; phase II funds the company, which "as a rule" is founded after phase I (GO-Bio [A]). *Inference:* no product revenue this decade; money comes from grants, venture capital and partner deals. The company may not be founded before late 2028.

**What you would do in the first 12 months (*inference*).** Map pharma partners working on Duchenne and gene editing; prepare a seed round (biotech needs tens of millions); manage patents with Helmholtz; support grant reporting. Investors will expect a biotech business-development background.

**Three questions to ask.**
1. "GO-Bio next phase I runs to Oct 2028. Do you plan to found earlier, for example with Helmholtz Enterprise, and when do you need the business person?"
2. "Is the plan your own Duchenne medicine or licensing the transporter to partners? Have partners asked to test it?"
3. "Who owns the patents (Helmholtz, TUM or both), and has a licence to a future company been discussed with the transfer office?"

**Status.** Nature paper 2 Sep 2026; GO-Bio next running; Helmholtz Munich (1 Dec 2025): the funding is "to prepare for the founding of a startup" ([IDG news](https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/idg/news-detail/funding-project-brings-new-technology-for-precise-therapeutic-delivery-closer-to-clinical-application) [B]). No company found. Helmholtz Enterprise 2026-2 winners are not published (checked 20 Nov 2026).

### 3. RadioGene-2 — PET tracking of cell and gene therapies (TUM Klinikum, Nuclear Medicine)
**What it does.** Living medicines (for example CAR-T cells against cancer) are given a harmless extra receptor. A radioactive tracer injected later binds only to that receptor, so a PET scan shows where the cells are and how many there are. In mice the tracer was cleared quickly through the kidneys and bound only to the labelled cells ([TUM, 13 Jun 2025](https://www.tum.de/aktuelles/alle-meldungen/pressemitteilungen/details/wie-sich-immunzellen-im-koerper-verfolgen-lassen) [B]; paper: Morath, Fritschle et al., *Nature Biomedical Engineering* 2025). Patent application filed; aim: "commercialise it for pharmaceutical research and approve it for clinical application … a spin-off and cooperation with a contract manufacturer … are planned" ([GO-Bio](https://go-bio.de/gobio/en/projects/go-bio-initial_eng/_documents/radiogene_eng.html) [A]). People: Volker Morath, Katja Fritschle; study lead Prof. Wolfgang Weber (TUM release).

**Who would buy, and what is paid today.** Developers of cell and gene therapies, first for animal studies, later in patient trials. CAR-T makes up 66 % of genetically modified cell therapies in development (Citeline Q2 2026 [B]). Precedent model: ImaginAb licenses and supplies its CD8 PET tracer to a CAR-T developer (Leucid Bio, Apr 2023, [Pharmaceutical Technology](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/news/imaginab-cd8-immunopet-tracer/) [B], >3 years old). No price for tracer supply or licences found `[UNVERIFIED]`.

**Competitors.** ImaginAb (CD8 PET tracer; raised $12.8m in Jun 2021, [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/imaginab-completes-12-8-million-financing-to-accelerate-growth-plans) [B], >3 years old). imuveo, from the same TUM clinic: CD2 PET tracer for immunotherapy monitoring, founded 2022, "1.6M € in undiluted funding from EXIST Forschungstransfer" ([TUM Venture Labs](https://www.tum-venture-labs.de/teams/imuveo/) [B]); the TUM release names imuveo as a related approach.

**Regulatory path and time to revenue.** Using radioactive substances on people for research needs a permit from the Federal Office for Radiation Protection (BfS), unless it only needs to be notified (§ 31 StrlSchG, [gesetze-im-internet](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/strlschg/__31.html) [A]), on top of the drug-trial approval; TUM says clinical trials must still test safety and efficacy (TUM release [B]). *Inference:* the receptor gene sits inside the partner's cell therapy, which is itself an ATMP (EMA [A]), so each clinical use depends on a partner's trial. The fastest revenue is research-use supply to pharma for animal studies (~2 years); clinical revenue is 5+ years away.

**What you would do in the first 12 months (*inference*).** Find 3–5 cell-therapy companies willing to test it in animal studies; set up the contract manufacturer the GO-Bio plan names; choose between selling a research product and licensing deals; write the next grant.

**Three questions to ask.**
1. "Who would pay first: pharma for animal studies, or hospitals for patient monitoring? Has any company tested it?"
2. "How do you differ from imuveo and ImaginAb, and could you work with imuveo instead of competing?"
3. "The feasibility phase ended on 30 Sep: which grant comes next, and is a business person part of that application?"

**Status.** No company and no 2026 news found. The GO-Bio page still shows the plan; the TUM release (Jun 2025) says commercialisation preparations "laufen derzeit".

### 4. FormulAIte — AI platform for RNA nanocarrier design (LMU, Pharmacy)
**What it does.** Software (ALINA, "Active Learning Inference for Nucleic Acids") that combines machine learning with molecular simulations to predict which fat- or polymer-based particles will carry an RNA medicine best, so fewer lab tests are needed. Funded by EXIST-Forschungstransfer since July 2026 (grant 03EFBY0380); milestones: refine models, "establishment of strategic partnerships", ALINA as a minimum viable product, "an initial pilot study", proof of concept ([Merkel lab](https://merkel.cup.uni-muenchen.de/exist-project/) [B]). Funding "approximately one million euros"; team Dr. Joschka Müller, Dr. Felix Sieber-Schäfer, Dr. Min Jiang, Florian Orbach; mentor Prof. Olivia Merkel; EFT pays "three researchers and one business specialist" ([LMU](https://www.lmu.de/en/newsroom/news-overview/news/million-euro-exist-funding-for-two-lmu-pharmacy-teams-5214c051.html) [B]). An ERC Proof of Concept grant adds **€150,000** for 1 Jan 2027–30 Jun 2028 ([CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101334799) [A]).

**Who would buy, and what is paid today.** RNA drug developers (1,370 RNA therapies in development, Citeline Q2 2026 [B]). The lab invites developers who "do not yet have a formulation strategy" to collaborate (Merkel lab [B]). Today developers license delivery technology (Genevant deals above [B]) or buy formulation work from contract labs, which mostly quote per project; no public price found `[UNVERIFIED]`.

**Competitors.** Mana.bio (AI-designed lipid nanoparticles): $19.5m seed, Oct 2023, led by Andreessen Horowitz Bio + Health and others ([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/mana-bio-advances-ai-based-lipid-nanoparticle-lnp-platform-to-accelerate-programmable-drug-delivery-for-rna-and-gene-therapies) [B], >3 years old). Genevant and contract formulation labs. Sister team cevovec (LMU, ~€1.2m EFT, RNA carriers) already names a business-development member (LMU [B]).

**Regulatory path and time to revenue (*inference*).** The platform is an R&D tool, so neither drug nor device rules apply to it; the customer's medicine is regulated. Paid pilot projects are possible within the EFT period (to about mid-2028).

**What you would do (*inference*).** The business seat is probably filled (EFT requires it). Role for you: later company phase, partnerships and sales, or as a contact. Ask, do not pitch for a seat.

**Three questions to ask.**
1. "Who holds the business-specialist post in your EFT team, and do you plan a bigger commercial team at founding?"
2. "Software licence, paid formulation projects, or your own drug candidates: which model do pilot partners prefer?"
3. "How is ALINA different from Mana.bio's AI-designed lipids?"

**Status.** EFT running since July 2026; ERC PoC from Jan 2027; no company yet found.

### 5. KIARA — AI in the operating room (Fraunhofer HHI, Berlin)
**What it does.** Cameras built into operating-room lamps plus AI recognise people, tools and steps in an operation and draft the operation report; surgeons check, complete and approve it. Original research project Aug 2022–Jul 2025 (BMBF), with Charité, Karl Leibinger Medizintechnik, Gebrüder Martin and HFC as partners ([HHI](https://hhi.fraunhofer.de/en/departments/vit/projects/kiara.html) [B]). The spin-out now pitches "real-time surgical workflow intelligence": automated documentation, live case visibility for OR managers, "a live OR pilot at a leading European university hospital" ([ad PDF](https://cms-cdn.lmu.de/media/04-iec/iec/downloads/kiara-cofounder-wanted-long-pdf.pdf) [B]).

**Who would buy, and what is paid today.** Hospitals (OR management, surgery departments). The ad claims manual documentation "costs hospitals an estimated €35,000 per surgeon per year" (citing TV-Ärzte/VKA 2024 and Deutsches Ärzteblatt 2017; company claim, not checked `[UNVERIFIED]`). Germany had about **16.5 million** inpatient operations in 2023 (procedure codes, not patients; [KBV](https://www.kbv.de/infothek/zahlen-und-fakten/gesundheitsdaten/ops-prozeduren-und-personal-in-krankenhaeusern) [B]). I found no separate reimbursement for OR documentation software, so it would come from hospital budgets *(inference)*.

**Competitors.** Caresyntax (Berlin/Boston; "Smart OR Box" collects OR data): $180m in Aug 2024, of which $100m equity ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2024/08/15/caresyntax-raises-180m-surgical-data-management-platform) [B]). Theator (surgical video AI): $15.5m Series A, Feb 2021 ([Insight Partners](https://www.insightpartners.com/ideas/theator-raises-15-5m-series-a-round-to-scale-its-surgical-intelligence-platform/) [B], >3 years old).

**Regulatory path and time to revenue.** Modules that "collect and maintain administrative patient data" or "store patient medical history" are not medical-device software; modules giving "expert assistance for clinical decision-making" are (MDCG 2019-11 rev.1 [A]). *Inference:* report drafting alone is probably not a medical device, so hospital pilots could pay within ~1–2 years; live alerts on "workflow errors" (ad) could make it class IIa or higher and add 2–3 years.

**What you would do in the first 12 months (*inference*, from the ad's job list).** Customer discovery with OR managers; turn the pilot into a paid contract; set price and sales process; lead grant and seed fundraising. Based in Germany (Berlin team).

**Three questions to ask.**
1. "Is the pilot paid, and who signed it: the OR manager, the surgery department or IT?"
2. "Does the product stay documentation-only, or will it give live alerts that make it a medical device?"
3. "Which grant funds the team now (EXIST, Fraunhofer programmes), and would you accept a co-founder without a VC track record who brings AI-implementation work?"

**Status.** Ad still on the LMU IEC board on 20 Nov 2026 (next to LUXONNIC, non-health). No company name, funding or launch news found.
